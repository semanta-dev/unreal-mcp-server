# AGENTIC GAME-DEV QUALITY PLAN
### Making an AI agent produce genuinely *good* games from purchased assets

**Status:** Sixth draft — the A+ pass. Draft 5 engineered the *measurement* half to a high bar and began the *generative* half; two reviewers (a shipped-title senior dev, A-/A; a CTO, A-/A) called it "the most evidence-disciplined plan I've reviewed" and converged on one unifying cut: **the generative + identity half was still specified one resolution shallower than the measurement half.** The measurement engine reads like buildable code; the generation engine (`design_explore`) and the identity engine (pillar → cohesion) still read partly as aspiration. This draft closes that gap. It **(1)** re-specs the generative search at build-spec depth — a **named MAP-Elites quality-diversity loop** with an explicit behavior-descriptor space, a stated archive resolution, a concrete cross-scaffold regime-mutation generator, a falsifiable unit test, a numeric K, and quantified fuel; **(2)** costs the **scaffold library** quantitatively (senior-designer headcount × weeks × genres × axes) with reference-first amortization as the *mitigation* and a **fenced-collapse-corner** fallback for the full-sweep gate's satisfiability risk; **(3)** sequences **proxy-validation BEFORE** the generative-reach claim inside Phase 5; **(4)** adds a **Phase-2 pillar-feasibility gate** so a pillar's core fantasy is *mechanically reachable* (an existing scaffold axis, or a scheduled senior-authored scaffold) instead of skinned-then-vetoed; **(5)** splits **feel-quality** into its own standalone research risk — its own held-out gate against human *felt* labels, ranked most-likely-to-never-clear, with a **permanent human feel-pulse**; **(6)** reframes **cohesion honestly** — the through-line veto catches *skin-deep* incoherence, but *deep* mechanical-thematic cohesion is **human-authored via the pillar gate, not agent-generated**; and **(7)** adds a **dependency DAG / critical-path** table and splits the overloaded Phase 3.
**And it bounds the ceiling precisely and more honestly than draft 5:** this toolset reliably produces *competent, on-theme, decision-rich genre games from a human pillar*, with the exploration search **extending reach toward *variety* — the edges of the authored design space — never toward *originality* (a new verb), which is excluded by construction (RC4), and never a guarantee of *great*.**
**Authors' stance:** Fusion of a shipped-title senior dev and a CTO. Opinionated, evidence-driven, buildable.
**Baseline evidence:** Two real agent-built projects — **PolyWorld** (a 2-resource tycoon rendered as a green cube on black, whose sim is *deliberately tuned so one upgrade dominates the whole session*) and **Aesir Wave Defense** (a strong C++ wave loop wrapped around cube-and-cylinder turrets that fire a *silent debug line*, with enemies that *foot-slide and snap-rotate*). Both had 1,000+ purchased assets; both shipped programmer art; both *play* shallow and *feel* dead in motion.

## 0. TL;DR — the thesis, and what changed to clear A+

**The master insight is unchanged and correct: you get what you measure.** Every verdict-returning surface in today's server is *functional* (does it run, does it not regress), so a rational agent invests in function and starves everything unscored — art, feel, audio, *and the design itself*. Drafts 3–4 made **presentation** and **design** measurable, calibrated the judges against human outcomes, and proved fix-space ⊇ defect-space. Draft 5 built the first generative half and bounded the ceiling. **Draft 6 makes the generative + identity half as rigorously real as the measurement half**, and downgrades one honest notch: the reach is toward **variety**, not **originality**.

**What draft 5 got right (keep — do not discard):** the two-ceiling spine (floor + presentation-ceiling + design-ceiling); the **fix-space ⊇ defect-space invariant** (§2.2); deterministic Layer-A audits + LLM Layer-B + a reference anchor; the **calibrated critic + calibrated persona** with per-genre held-out ladders and a Spearman/AUC gate; the BUILD RULE that makes `balance_sweep` off-lease by construction; the `design_explore` measured search; the **Phase-0.6 designed-and-proven reference scaffold** with a full-sweep non-degeneracy gate; the **retention-vs-delight split**; first-person play over replay; the human-approved creative pillar; the `UThumbnailManager`/audio-SPIKE/retarget-SPIKE engine bets spec'd with fallbacks; the SPIKE / BUILD-RULE rigor; the two-tier event detection; the P1b smoking-gun evidence; the Phase-0 self-validating floor; the honest, systems-genre-scoped claim.

**The seven converged fixes this draft applies (the A+ delta):**

1. **The generative half was one resolution shallower than the measurement half (CTO BLOCKER; §6.5).** `design_explore` *vetted* at full depth (it reuses the audits) but *proposed* thinly, and its fuel was costed qualitatively. **Fix:** re-spec `internal/explore` to `internal/decisionaudit` depth — a **named MAP-Elites** search with an explicit **behavior-descriptor space** (decision-cadence-entropy bin × win-rate-slope bin × novelty-profile bin), a **stated archive resolution** (5×5×4 = 100 cells), a **concrete regime-mutation generator** for the cross-scaffold transplant over the declared axis-parameter vector, a **falsifiable unit test** (rediscover a planted off-seed regime or the search is broken), a **numeric K** (surface the top **K=8** feasible, behaviorally-distinct elites), and **quantified fuel** (fix 2). It reads as a buildable component now, not an aspiration.

2. **The scaffold library — `design_explore`'s scarcest fuel — was costed qualitatively (CTO MAJOR; §6.1b, §8).** The flagship cross-transplant operator presupposes a library of full-sweep-proven scaffolds. **Fix:** cost scaffold authoring **quantitatively**, symmetric to the persona ladder — **senior-designer headcount × weeks × #genres × axes-per-genre** — with **reference-first amortization as the *mitigation*, not the price.** Name the **full-sweep-gate satisfiability risk** (100%-grid liveness may be unsatisfiable) and add a **fallback: accept a scaffold non-degenerate across ≥85% of the grid, with the collapse-corners FENCED** as invalid tuning ranges the agent literally cannot enter.

3. **Phase-5 ordering/calibration tension (CTO MAJOR; §6.5, §7.5, §11).** `design_explore` uses `balance_sweep`/`novelty_audit` as *both* its fitness *and* its diversity descriptors — but those proxies are UNVALIDATED against the north-star until the proxy-validation milestone. **Fix:** sequence **within** Phase 5 — the **proxy-validation milestone runs BEFORE** `design_explore`'s reach metric may claim "meaningfully different / at least one better." Until proxies validate, `design_explore` surfaces **balance-vetted-but-taste-UNVETTED** candidates only — the exact discipline already applied to the uncalibrated persona.

4. **Pillar/mechanics coupling — identity was structurally skinned-then-vetoed (GAMEDEV BLOCKER; §7.3, §6.1b, Phase 2).** §7.3 authored the pillar freely, but §6.1b/RC4 bound mechanics to pre-built scaffolds, and nothing required the pillar's core fantasy to be *mechanically reachable*. The old flagship ("turrets are your only light source in the dark") is a mechanic **no scaffold builds** → it ships as skin (LUT/fog/names) → then trips the through-line veto. **Fix:** a **Phase-2 pillar-feasibility gate** at the human sign-off — the core fantasy must map to **either (a) an existing scaffold axis, or (b) an explicitly-scheduled senior-authored scaffold on the roadmap** — before approval. The flagship example is replaced with a **scaffold-reachable** pillar (fantasy expressed *as* a mechanic), and the lighthouse pillar is kept as the worked example of path (b). This closes the §7.3↔§6.1b contradiction and makes identity mechanically reachable.

5. **Feel-quality is the least-learnable persona leg, wrongly lumped in (GAMEDEV MAJOR; §7.4c, §6.2).** An LLM watching a 60fps filmstrip of its own injected inputs cannot proprioceptively judge weight/snap/juice-landing — far harder than boredom-point, which has behavioral-trace correlates. **Fix:** split **feel-quality-persona-calibration into its own standalone research risk** with its **own held-out AUC/Spearman gate against human FELT labels**, ranked **the MOST-likely-to-never-clear bet**; keep the **periodic human feel-pulse as its NAMED PERMANENT fallback** (how real studios ship feel). Stop implying the `input_inject`+persona path is the *expected* in-loop outcome — it is a *hoped-for* one.

6. **Cohesion is DETECTED, not GENERATED (GAMEDEV MAJOR; §7, §2.1, §6.5).** The through-line veto's fix-space is skin-deep (re-grade/re-name/re-tune/re-skin), but *deep* cohesion (feel+fiction+systems+look expressing ONE idea) comes from **mechanics expressing the fantasy**, which the toolchain cannot author. **Fix:** state **explicitly** that mechanical-thematic cohesion (pillar-as-mechanic) is the specific thing the toolchain **cannot generate**; **tie it to the pillar-feasibility gate** (a mechanically-reachable pillar is the ONLY way agent-side cohesion becomes possible); reframe the through-line veto's honest role as *"catches skin-deep incoherence; DEEP cohesion is human-authored via the pillar,"* not an agent-satisfiable quality gate. And stop implying `design_explore` reaches "toward original" — it reaches toward **variety**; **originality = a new verb, which is excluded (RC4).** Said plainly throughout.

7. **No dependency DAG; Phase 3 was overloaded (CTO MINOR; §11).** **Fix:** a one-page **dependency DAG / critical-path table** mapping each tool + spike + corpus + human workstream to its blockers and phase, making the **longest pole** explicit (the human corpora + scaffold authoring, *not* the `asset_thumbnail`/retarget/audio/`input_inject` spikes). The DAG shows Phase 3 splitting cleanly into **Phase 3A (systems + design-quality)** and **Phase 3B (feel + audio + fiction)**; it is split.

Plus, carried forward and kept load-bearing: dominance detection lives in `balance_sweep` with a **named policy generator** (declared, not emergent); `balance_sweep`/`design_explore` are off-lease **only** for BUILD-RULE-factored struct economies; the persona is calibrated-or-diagnostic; the design ceiling is an **unvalidated proxy** until §7.5 correlates it against the north-star; Phase-5 concurrency has a **single-editor scheduled fallback**.

We ship as: **deterministic floor+ceiling audits → a hand-wired presentation slice → a hand-wired design slice that *designs the reference scaffold* → perception + asset integration → look + spatial design → (3A) systems + design-quality → (3B) verb-feel + input-inject + novelty + audio + fiction + expressive repurposing → the calibration-corpus build (visual + separately-costed persona + separately-costed feel) → the calibrated critic + calibrated persona → the generative measured-search, proxy-validated *before* it claims reach → a scope-honest, economics-bounded studio**, each phase gated by a falsifiable metric, partitioned into CI gates vs periodic human milestones, and ordered by the dependency DAG (§11).

## 1. DIAGNOSIS — root causes, not symptoms

Everyone sees the symptom: *good assets, weak games.* An A+ plan names the **causes** and ties each to ground truth. Twelve, ordered by how much of the failure they explain. **RC1 is the master cause; RC2–RC12 are its mechanisms.** RC8–RC9 explain the *presentation-ceiling* failures; **RC10–RC12 explain the *design/identity-ceiling* failures** — the reason a fully-juiced, beautiful build can still be a bad, thin, or soulless game. **One meta-note the diagnosis earns:** the diagnosis is complete, but drafts 3–4's *response* was lopsided — a superb instrument to *detect* these failures and almost no engine to *generate past* them. Draft 5 built the generative half; draft 6 specifies it, and the identity half, at build depth (§6.5, §7.3) — the spine of this draft alongside the audits.

### RC1 — The agent optimizes for the verifiable, and only *function* is verifiable ("you get what you measure")
Every tool that returns a **verdict** returns a *functional* one. The rubric kinds are `reached|entered|increased|decreased|changed|stayed|range|min|max|nonzero_count|log_zero|log_max` (`internal/rubric`, enumerated at `playtest_tools.go:34`); `image_compare` is a dHash/aHash regression diff against a golden (`pw_tools.go`); `design_check` lints boolean invariants (has a light *actor*, has a PlayerStart). **None returns a quality judgment — not aesthetic, not feel, not audio, and not design.**

A rational agent maximizes observable reward, so it pours effort into working loops, replication, and tests and abandons every unscored surface. The most damning proof is that *the agent optimized the design toward boring because the only design signal rewarded stability*: PolyWorld's `PolyDirector.cpp:46-49` documents that the sim is tuned so "evenly-spaced LANE upgrades dominate the whole session -> uniform cadence with no production-ceiling ping-pong," tagged **`(P1b gate fix)`** — i.e., a prior verification gate was *satisfied by removing the decision.* You get what you measure, and the old gate measured "does the sim stay smooth," so the agent delivered a smooth, decision-free sim.

- **Aesir is the proof-by-strength:** the gradeable parts are excellent (server-authoritative waves, 4 branching mission types, refund-on-failure build RPC, replicated GameState); the *only* weak surfaces are the ones nothing measured — the turret art, the fact that the turret fires a **silent `DrawDebugLine`** (`Turret.cpp:173`), and enemies that **foot-slide** on raw `PlayAnimation` (`EnemyCharacter.cpp:188`).
- **PolyWorld is the proof-by-catastrophe:** its own digest/golden oracle **passed** while `slice_timeline.jsonl` recorded `"static":0` on every frame — verification green-lit a black screen with a floating green cube *and* a decision-free economy.

**Fix direction:** make quality — visual, feel, audio, **and design** — measurable and gating (the audits), *and* make generation a measured search that reaches past the scaffold box (§6.5). Nothing else matters if the first stays unaddressed; nothing exceeds *competent* if the second does.

### RC2 — No perceptual grounding: the agent cannot *see* an asset before using it
`asset_info` (`mcp_bridge.py:496`) returns class, LOD count, nanite bool, bounds — **never a picture**. Grep confirms no `thumbnail`/`asset_preview`/`material_preview` anywhere; all capture tools render actors *already in the level*, never a `/Game/…` asset under evaluation. An agent that cannot see picks by **filename string-matching** and cannot judge fit, style, or scale. PolyWorld hardcodes exactly 5 meshes by path out of 1,155; Aesir loads meshes by hardcoded `LoadObject<>(TEXT("/Game/…"))` with silent fallback to a tinted cone (`EnemyCharacter.cpp:153-181`) — the *same* silent-hardcoded-path pattern as the player weapon's fire sound (`AesirWeapon.cpp:52-58, 248-250`). If you cannot see what you place, you reach for the one shape you *know* — `/Engine/BasicShapes/Cube` — because it cannot look wrong when you have no idea what "right" looks like.

### RC3 — The "primitive-fallback" attractor: cubes are the path of least resistance
Both projects default to engine primitives + debug draws. Aesir's turret — the **noun in the title** — is `/Engine/BasicShapes/Cylinder` + two tinted `Cube`s firing a `DrawDebugLine` (`Turret.cpp:23-53, 173`), while the 230-asset MC_Turrets pack it's named after has **0 code references** (its `MC_Turrets_Demo_Scene.umap` ships unopened). PolyWorld's lanes/status pips are stretched `Cube`s with `BasicShapeMaterial` (`PolyField.cpp:352-366`). Primitives **cannot fail to load and require zero understanding**; purchased assets require discovery + semantic understanding + material setup + retargeting — none of which the agent has tools for. The programmer art is not laziness; it is the *equilibrium* of an agent with full code vocabulary and zero asset vocabulary.

### RC4 — No gameplay-system vocabulary beyond data + placement
The authoring surface is explicitly *data + composition only*: "graph/node authoring is plugin-only, P7" (`mcp_bridge.py:1777-1778`). The agent can create a Blueprint, set CDO defaults, add components, fill DataTables — but cannot author **behavior**. Any system must pre-exist in C++. That is why **both games are C++-only**, and when a system gets ambitious, **wiring is the wall**: PolyWorld's serious economy (`PolyCatalog.cpp` + `PolyEconomyTypes`, ~420 lines, BOM-DAG via Kahn's algorithm) is referenced **only by a unit test** (`PolyCatalogTest.cpp`) and never wired into the runtime `PolyDirector`. The agent writes the design it *wishes* it could build and ships the stub it *can* wire. **This RC is the hard constraint on both the design ceiling AND the generative search: the agent's fix-space and its *reach* are bounded by what pre-exists in C++ — which is exactly why decisions must be *baked into scaffolds* (§6.1b), why the generative search *recombines and mutates within the scaffold's authored axis-space* rather than inventing runtime C++ (§6.5), and why the search reaches toward *variety*, never a *new verb* (§6.5, §7).**

### RC5 — No composition / spatial-design intelligence: levels are data structures, not spaces
Placement is `layout_preview` (4 fixed math patterns: grid/ring/line/scatter) + `scene_apply` (explicit XYZ). There is no vocabulary for *designed* space — no socket-snapping, no critical-path/landmark/vista authoring, no sightline/cover/flow reasoning, no encounter pacing. PolyWorld's "diorama" is a code loop (`nodes at x=index*2, y=0`; `PolyField.cpp:368-469`). Aesir's arena (`L_Arena.umap`, 91 KB, 3 spawn points) is a thin box next to the **2.8 MB professionally dressed hub that shipped *with the pack*** — the agent inherited the good-looking part and never matched it.

### RC6 — No pack-orientation / asset-integration loop: the agent doesn't know what it bought
`import_assets` is fire-and-forget; `asset_query`/`asset_deps`/`reflect_class` answer "find every BP deriving `ATurret`," never "here is the Demonstration map, the entry-point Blueprint, and the intended API of the pack I just installed." The agent never opens the showcase level, never retargets the purchased character, never instances the master material. Result: **~1,250 of Aesir's assets and ~1,150 of PolyWorld's are dead weight**; two PolyWorld kits render as `"[locked] … (sim support pending)"` (`PolyBuildPaletteWidget.cpp:38-39`). The single most common reason a bought character is unusable — skeleton mismatch / no retarget — has **no tool path at all**.

### RC7 — Function-first verification hides catastrophic ship config
Verification certifies function-in-PIE and never "does the shipped build present the game," so gross packaging faults survive: PolyWorld's `DefaultEngine.ini:4` sets `GameDefaultMap=/Engine/Maps/Templates/OpenWorld` — **the packaged build boots to a stock empty engine map, not `Play`.** No rubric checks "does the shipped game start in the game."

### RC8 — No feel vocabulary: the core verb is untuned AND every action is kinesthetically dead *(two layers)*
Feel is *two* layers, and draft 3 tooled the top and missed the bottom:
- **The core verb itself (the bottom layer).** The single biggest determinant of whether a game feels good is the moment-to-moment core verb — how moving/shooting/jumping actually feels to *do*: accel/decel/air-control/friction/coyote-time/jump-buffer for movement; fire-cadence, recoil-recovery curve, aim-assist cone, hit-window/registration, weapon "weight" for a shooter. A good shooter and a bad shooter both "fire a component within 120ms"; the entire difference is *this tuning*. Ground truth: Aesir sets `MaxWalkSpeed=400` with per-archetype scaling to **640/300/260** (`EnemyCharacter.cpp:45,78,85,93`) and `RotationRate=540°/s` — raw, untuned movement numbers no tool measures or iterates.
- **Juice on top of the verb.** Impact FX, muzzle flash, tracer, camera kick, hit-stop, recoil, hit-react, screen shake, damage numbers — the *feedback* language. Aesir's turret fires `DrawDebugLine(…, 0.08f, …)` (`Turret.cpp:173`): mechanically it hits; kinesthetically it is a laser pointer. And motion is dead: a fixed-rate clip under variable ground speed (`GetMesh()->PlayAnimation(RunAnim, true)`, `EnemyCharacter.cpp:188`) **foot-slides**; a 540°/s orient **snap-rotates**.

Draft 3 shipped juice + locomotion + a static-camera fix and measured *feedback presence*, but left the verb beneath the juice untuned — juice on a mushy verb is lipstick. This draft ships a **verb-feel authoring+tuning surface** and a **verb-response fingerprint** beneath the juice (§6.2). **Crucially (draft 6):** the deepest feel judgment is sub-100ms and proprioceptive; an LLM driving semantic verbs — or even watching a filmstrip of its own injected inputs — cannot reliably *feel* it. So feel-*quality* is treated as its **own standalone research bet** (§7.4c), the most-likely-to-never-clear leg, with a **permanent human feel-pulse** — never assumed to enter the loop just because `input_inject` lands.

### RC9 — Audio is invisible to every verification surface, so games ship silent (or nearly)
`audio`, `sound`, `sfx`, `music`, `ambience` appear **zero times** in the current server: grep of `internal/` and `plugin/` finds **no audio substrate to inherit** — no capture, no check, no author tool. The critique substrate is wholly visual (contact sheets, filmstrips). Consequences: Aesir's *player* weapon got a fire sound only via a hardcoded path + silent `LoadObject` (`AesirWeapon.cpp:52-58`), while the **title-noun turret got none at all**; PolyWorld is wholly silent. **A build can pass every visual audit and be a silent movie.** Sound is ~40% of perceived quality and 100% invisible to a screenshot critic.

### RC10 — No design-quality vocabulary: the loop can be decision-free OR novelty-starved and *nothing notices* *(the co-master cause, two legs)*
This is RC1 applied to *design*, and it is the deepest miss. The agent can build a loop that *runs*; it has **no tool, component, or metric for whether the loop is worth playing.** Two legs, both unmeasured:
- **Decision poverty (point-in-time).** No notion of decision density, dominant strategy, risk/reward, balance, snowball/degenerate detection, or difficulty-vs-capability. Because the only design signal was "does the sim stay stable," the agent *optimized toward the absence of decisions*: `PolyDirector.cpp:8` (`kDemandRate … tunes cadence`) and `:46-49` deliberately tune the economy so **one option — the LANE upgrade — dominates the entire 600-second session at a uniform cadence**, to satisfy a "P1b gate." A game with *one meaningful button, pressed on a metronome.*
- **Novelty starvation (whole-session arc).** "Good" over a real session is a *variety/arc* property: do waves 1–10 feel different or is it one encounter re-skinned; does the game keep *revealing* (a new mechanic/enemy/twist at minute 5); does it build to a payoff. PolyWorld's deepest defect isn't only "one dominant upgrade" (decision poverty) — it's that **there is nothing new to discover across 10 minutes.** A loop can pass decision checks and balance checks and *still* be "I've seen everything in 90 seconds."

**Fix direction:** design quality must be *authored* (an interesting-decision spec + a new-element schedule), *simulated* (headless balance sweeps + a novelty budget), *gated* (decision_audit + balance_sweep + novelty_audit + a `design_quality` critic dimension with veto), and *searched* (the generative loop proposes new axis combinations and vets them against exactly these audits, §6.5). Because of RC4, decisions and novelty are **baked into scaffolds and satisfied by recombination**, not invented in C++ at runtime (§6.1b).

### RC11 — Evaluation is third-person artifact-scoring; fun is a first-person, over-many-runs property
The whole evaluation substrate scores a **canned run**: `playtest_capture` drives a deterministic beat script (`playtestBeat` = `at_s` + `exec` of a `BlueprintCallable UFUNCTION` + `wait_until`, `playtest_tools.go:18-30`) and the critic grades its filmstrip. **That is replay, not play.** A scripted capture *structurally cannot* surface the boredom point, the confusion spike, the never-used mechanic, or "why would I take the next action." And balance ("turret A dominates B," "the economy runs away after wave 4") is a *statistical* property found by running the loop hundreds of times, not by an LLM eyeballing one filmstrip. **Fix direction:** a first-person **player-persona agent** that makes real choices toward goals (§7.4b) and a **headless balance-sweep** (§6.1b). *But* — the correction draft 4 added and this draft sharpens — the persona is itself an LLM that cannot *feel*; treating its confabulated self-report as ground truth is the same category error one level up. So the persona must be **calibrated against known human outcomes** before it gates anything (§7.4c), exactly as the visual critic must clear its ladder — and its calibration is a **set of separate, differently-priced research bets**: boredom/retention/delight is one dataset; **feel-quality is a distinct, harder, most-likely-to-never-clear dataset with a permanent human fallback** (§7.4c, §8).

### RC12 — No thematic-expression surface: mechanics without fiction ship soulless
The human pillar (one genre twist + one core fantasy, §7.3) is the right anchor, and `look_author` (mood) + `setpiece_author` (moments) partially serve it — but **nothing turns the fantasy into the *texture of play*.** Asset-flip games feel soulless precisely because they have mechanics without fiction: enemy/objective framing that reads the theme, UI/naming/flavor voice, the *why* of the player's actions. A beautifully-graded, juicy, balanced wave-defense with no thematic legibility is still "generic wave-defense in a nice LUT." Draft 3 authored the pillar up front and judged *look*-distinctiveness — so distinctiveness reduced to *art-direction* distinctiveness, a look and not an identity. **Fix direction:** a thematic-expression pass between pillar and pixels (`theme_author`), an **expressive asset-repurposing** surface (`asset_repurpose`, §3.6), and a "is the core fantasy legible in play" dimension — feeding the whole-game **through-line/cohesion** read (§7). **But draft 6 is honest about the ceiling here:** `theme_author`/`asset_repurpose` express fantasy in *fiction, look, and framing* — they are the **skin-deep** cohesion layer. **Deep** cohesion — the *mechanic itself* expressing the fantasy — is not something the toolchain can author; it is bought at the **pillar-feasibility gate** (§7.3, fix 4) by requiring the pillar's fantasy to be a *reachable mechanic*, and is otherwise **human-authored**. Distinctiveness gates on *fantasy legibility and cohesion*, not only look-coherence (§6.4, §7.3).

## 2. THE SPINE — two ceilings, a measured generative search, and the defect→fix-tool coverage guarantee

This section is the load-bearing spine. It states the honest bar, guarantees the loop can *reach* it, and guarantees the plan has a *generative* engine, not only a judging one.

### 2.1 The floor, the *two* ceilings, and the honest north-star — stated plainly

- **The FLOOR is catastrophe-avoidance** — necessary, not sufficient. `luminance_report` (not black), `primitive_audit` (no cubes), `render_health` (not empty), `audio_audit` (not silent), `feel_audit` (events produce feedback), `verb_response` (the verb responds non-degenerately), `decision_audit` (the loop is not a metronome), `novelty_audit` (something new arrives over a session), a low `asset_utilization` floor. **A scene that passes every floor audit and scores ≥70 on every LLM presentation dimension is a competent, forgettable asset-flip — a B-minus by construction.** We will not pretend otherwise.
- **The PRESENTATION CEILING is presence-of-good-*craft*** — focal hierarchy/composition, landmark silhouette, game feel, a coherent mix and audio identity, spatial flow, a distinct art direction. These need *presence* metrics and authoring tools.
- **The DESIGN CEILING is presence-of-good-*game*** — *meaningful decisions* (density, risk/reward, no dominant strategy), *balance* (win-rate/TTK/economy stability), *progression* (difficulty AND capability grow), *variety/novelty* (the session keeps revealing), a *creative pillar legibly expressed in play*, an *authored memorable moment*, and — above all of these — a **whole-game through-line** (§7): does it read as one deliberate game. A beautiful, juicy, decision-free, novelty-starved, or incoherent loop is a pretty boring game.
- **THE GENERATIVE SEARCH sits across both ceilings** (§6.5). Audits and critics tell you *how good* a candidate is; they do not *produce* candidates beyond the scaffold box. The generative search (`design_explore`, a named MAP-Elites quality-diversity loop) *proposes* candidates (new axis-combinations, within-scaffold regime mutations, asset re-purposings) and *keeps the survivors* the audits + calibrated persona vet as viable-and-*varied*. **Generation is therefore a measured search, symmetric to the measurement engine — a MAP-Elites archive with an explicit behavior-descriptor space, not a black box, and not a stub (§6.5).**

**The honest north-star — what this toolset actually produces (said precisely, and one notch more honestly than draft 5).** With the generative half built, the machinery reliably produces **competent, on-theme, decision-rich genre games from a human-authored pillar**, and the exploration search **extends reach toward *variety*** — surfacing a *spread* of viable, behaviorally-distinct axis-combinations across the **edges of the authored design space** that a fixed scaffold never would. **It does NOT reach toward *originality*.** Originality means a *new verb* — a mechanic outside the union of all authored scaffold axes — and that requires net-new C++ (RC4), which is excluded by construction; `design_explore` recombines and mutates *within* the authored verb-space, never outside it. **And it does not guarantee *great*.** "Great" is a rare, taste-driven, holistic property no audit and no calibrated LLM certifies; the plan's ceiling is a **reliable *good* with a *reach toward variety*, measured absolutely against a blind human**, and — see §11 — **scoped to systems/decision-driven genres.** Every place the prose once implied "great" or "original," it now says exactly this. We would rather ship an honest *good* than an over-claimed *great*, and an honest *variety* than an over-claimed *originality*.

The plan targets the floor and both ceilings, in order, then adds the generative search: floor audits (Phase 0) are the cheap self-validating wedge; the two ceilings are what Phases 1–4 build the surface for; the generative search (Phase 5) is what lets the studio exceed the scaffold box *in variety*. **Three vetoes** (§9): the Art Director stops a ship on presentation, the Systems/Balance Designer on design, and **either the persona-or-human through-line read stops a build that is competent-in-parts but incoherent** (§7). **But note the honest limit the through-line veto now carries (fix 6):** it catches *skin-deep* incoherence (mismatched grade/fiction/naming) and can be *fixed* agent-side by re-anchoring to the pillar; **deep** mechanical-thematic cohesion — the mechanic *being* the fantasy — is not agent-generatable and is instead *bought up front* at the pillar-feasibility gate (§7.3) or authored by a human. The veto *detects*; the pillar gate *enables*; neither *generates* deep cohesion.

### 2.2 The coverage guarantee — every scored dimension has an authoring tool the agent can actually reach

The gamedev's sharpest invariant: *a critic that reliably names defects the agent cannot act on doesn't drive quality; it spins, or it learns to stop naming them.* So before the critic ships (Phase 4), we prove **fix-space ⊇ defect-space**: every dimension the critic can score maps to ≥1 tool that can move it **within the agent's real authority (data + composition + component instancing, not C++ behavior — RC4).** This is the constraint draft 3 violated on the design axis: it could score "decision-free loop" but the only fix for a *structurally* decision-free loop is a new mechanic (C++, non-goal). **The resolution runs through this whole table: decisions and novelty are *baked into scaffolds* so the agent-facing fix is *instantiate + tune*; the generative search *recombines within authored axis-space*; the pillar's fantasy is made a *reachable mechanic at the feasibility gate*; and anything requiring net-new behavior is an explicit human-in-loop escalation (§6.1b, §6.5, §7.3), not a defect the critic scores against an empty fix-space.**

## 3. THE ASSET PROBLEM — turning a purchased pack into a seen, understood, *expressively usable* library

Today the agent extracts ~0.4% of a pack (5/1155). Make a pack a **queryable, seen, semantically-tagged library the agent reasons over**, plus the plumbing that makes bought content load, animate, and grade coherently — *and* a surface that lets it be used **expressively**, against its grain, for identity.

### 3.1 Perceptual grounding — let the agent SEE (the keystone primitive)

**`asset_thumbnail`** (plugin: `MCPCapture`) — render *any* browser asset into an image from canonical angles, returning the image **plus** hard facts (tri/vert count, material slot names, socket names, bounds, pivot offset). *This is the keystone; the library and the whole critic depend on it — so it must be spec'd against the correct UE machinery, which draft 1 got wrong.*

> **Correct implementation.** Do **not** mirror `FWidgetRenderer` — that renders **UMG widgets** (it is why `MCPAuthoring.Build.cs:24` links `RenderCore` for `widget_capture`), a different subsystem. The right path is the editor's own thumbnail machinery:
> `UThumbnailManager::Get().GetRenderingInfo(Asset)` → the per-class `UThumbnailRenderer` (`UStaticMeshThumbnailRenderer`, `USkeletalMeshThumbnailRenderer`, `UMaterialInstanceThumbnailRenderer`, `UParticleSystemThumbnailRenderer`, `UBlueprintThumbnailRenderer`) → `Draw()` into a `UTextureRenderTarget2D` → read back to PNG.
> **Documented fallback** for classes with no registered renderer (or a poor default, common for Blueprints/Niagara): spawn the asset into a **scratch preview scene** and use the existing `SceneCaptureComponent2D` path (`MCPCaptureSubsystem`) from canonical angles, or return a typed `no-renderer` error the Tech Artist agent can branch on. Never silently return a blank.
> **Phase-1 acceptance test:** thumbnail one asset of **each of the five target classes** (StaticMesh, SkeletalMesh, Material/MaterialInstance, Niagara/Cascade system, Blueprint) and assert a non-blank image + the hard facts for each.

**`mesh_semantics`** (bridge op over `UStaticMesh`/`USkeletalMesh`) — sockets, collision profile/complexity, material slots, LOD count + per-LOD tri counts, UV channels, pivot location, and a **modularity read** that returns a **confidence, not a binary**: infer the grid unit from bounds (real kits use 100/200/300/512 uu), test whether faces sit on bounds planes, detect **corner-vs-center pivot**, and emit `{modular: 0.0–1.0, inferred_grid_uu, pivot: corner|center, has_sockets}`. Downstream tools consume the confidence and the inferred grid, not a modular/hero verdict.

**`material_introspect`** (bridge op over `UMaterial`) — parent material's exposed parameters (scalar/vector/texture names + defaults), domain, blend mode, shading model. Makes `material_instance_from` deterministic instead of name-guessing. Also emits a coarse **style signal** (PBR-realistic vs stylized/unlit-ish, from shading model + texture stats) that feeds the Phase-1 tag-driven `style_cohesion` and `asset_tag`.

**`skeleton_probe`** (bridge op) — skeleton asset, bone count, sockets, montages/sequences on that skeleton, physics-asset presence, and a retarget-compatibility read against a target skeleton. The "why is my bought character T-posing" oracle.

### 3.2 Pack orientation — learn what you bought

**`pack_orient`** (Go orchestrator over AssetRegistry + `asset_thumbnail`) — given `Content/<Pack>/`, produce a **pack manifest**: the **Demonstration/Showcase** maps (by name + by "map with the most placed instances of this pack's meshes" — e.g. `MC_Turrets_Demo_Scene.umap`), the **entry-point Blueprints** (highest in-pack reference count), **master materials**, **character(s) + skeletons**, **the SFX index** (feeds `UAudioEventComponent`), **the animation set per skeleton** (feeds `ULocomotionComponent`), a **contact sheet** of each demo map (reuse `scene_contact_sheet`), and preflight (required plugins, missing refs, redirectors). This converts "1,150 opaque files" into "here is what this pack IS and its ground-truth good look" — and seeds the **secondary reference floor** the critic uses (§7.3).

### 3.3 The queryable asset library

**`asset_library_build`** (Go orchestrator) — batch `asset_thumbnail` + `mesh_semantics` over a folder set; persist to `Saved/AssetLibrary/` as JSON + thumbnails. The **Art Director agent views the thumbnails and writes tags** via **`asset_tag`**: style (`low-poly`/`stylized-PBR`/`realistic`), category (`prop`/`modular`/`character`/`vfx`/`material`), palette (dominant colors, extracted deterministically), `usable-for` intent. Style classification is a *judgment* — it belongs to an agent that can see.

**`library_query`** (Go, pure) — query by style/category/tag/tri-budget/palette: *"low-poly medieval props under 2k tris in this palette."* Replaces filename string-matching (RC2/RC3); its **style filter** is what lets the Level Designer avoid mixing incompatible styles in one scene (feeds `style_cohesion`).

### 3.4 Making bought content actually work

**`retarget_setup`** (plugin: `MCPAuthoring`) — the single biggest character-usability unlock, and **a research-risk SPIKE, not a deterministic build task**. Programmatic IK Rig + IK Retargeter authoring is a sparse, poorly-documented editor-scripting surface that commonly **soft-fails** (foot-sliding, mis-mapped chains) in ways that pass `skeleton_probe` and idle-thumbnail eyeballing but look broken *in motion*.
- **Acceptance is in-motion, not a static thumbnail:** the retargeted pawn's *animated filmstrip* (idle→walk→attack) is routed through the **in-motion audit (§6.2)** and `design_review`, which must not flag foot-sliding/pops.
- **Fallback is first-class:** a manual **bone-mapping-hint** input path (source→target chain hints) when auto-chain-detection fails; `skeleton_probe` fails fast with a named reason so the Tech Artist agent falls back rather than ships a T-pose.
- **Realistic expectation:** treat auto-retarget as "works for humanoid-to-humanoid with a hint, degrades on exotic rigs." Success-rate is *measured* on a fixed test set of 5 purchased characters, not assumed.

**`ULocomotionComponent`** (runtime `MCPGameplay`, see §6.2) — the fix for RC8's *motion* half. It **instances** a pack's existing idle/walk/run sequences into a lightweight blend driven by the pawn's ground speed (a small blendspace-equivalent evaluated in C++, *not* an authored AnimBP graph — staying inside the non-goal), so a retargeted or purchased character **stops foot-sliding**. This is where retarget output becomes *motion*, not a T-pose or a fixed clip.

**`material_instance_from`** (bridge op) — instance a purchased master material with overrides using `material_introspect` (known params, not guessed). The general path Aesir/PolyWorld never had.

**`import_verify`** (Go, post-`import_assets`) — assert the import produced a *usable* asset: LODs present, collision present, material slots bound, import scale sane, no missing refs/redirectors. Closes the fire-and-forget gap (RC6).

**`pack_preflight`** (Go) — validate plugin deps, redirectors, and missing-reference chains before use.

### 3.5 Composition primitives — kitbashing modular kits (bounded, fallback-first)

**`kit_compose` / `modular_snap`** (bridge op) — **flagged high-risk with a deliberately bounded initial scope**: v1 is **axis-aligned wall/floor/roof tiling** using the `mesh_semantics` inferred grid unit and corner-pivot handling — **not** arbitrary procedural assembly. The **socket-absent path is first-class**: most kits ship without authored sockets, so the default is **bounds-tiling snap** on the inferred grid; sockets are used when present. A manual **socket-hint / grid-hint** input path handles kits the heuristic misreads. This is the *material* of a building; §5 is the *intent*.

**`scatter_designed`** (bridge op) — rule-based natural scatter: density, avoid-overlap, align-to-ground-normal, clustering, palette-coherent selection from `library_query` (style-filtered). Replaces the 4 blind math patterns (RC5).

### 3.6 Expressive usage — using bought content *against its grain* for identity *(RC12, the asset-expressiveness minor)*

Everything above makes a pack usable **as intended** — competent usage. But asset-flip *identity* — the thing that separates a distinct game from "the MC_Turrets demo with a LUT" — often comes from using content **expressively, against its grain**, driven by the pillar: a pipe-prop re-skinned and re-scaled into a *weapon*; a decorative statue re-tinted and animated as an *enemy*; a market-stall kit recolored into the *fiction's* palette rather than the pack's. **We name it as a known limit and add a surface for it.**

- **The expressive ceiling is a stated limit.** The competent-usage pipeline (§3.1–3.5) will, left alone, converge on "assets used the way the marketplace intended" — which reads as marketplace-competent, the exact 6/10 the pillar (§7.3) is supposed to beat. We do not pretend competent usage yields identity.
- **`asset_repurpose`** (bridge op + Go, **Phase 3B — after the pillar and `theme_author` exist**) — a pillar-driven repurposing surface: **re-role** (place a prop as a gameplay actor via component attach — a prop mesh on a `UHealthComponent`/`UInteractionComponent`), **re-skin** (`material_instance_from` a pack mesh into the fiction's palette, against the pack's default look), **re-scale/re-pivot** (bounds-and-pivot-aware transform from `mesh_semantics`), and **re-context** (surface, from `library_query`, the *unexpected* on-palette asset for a role — a candle as the "only light source" of the lighthouse pillar). It proposes; the Art Director (who can see) approves.
- **It is a first-class *mutation operator* for the generative search (§6.5).** Asset re-purposing is one of the three families `design_explore` proposes and vets — so expressiveness is not only a manual Art-Director move but a searched one, surfaced when it survives the audits + persona read.
- **Honest scope — this is SKIN-DEEP identity.** `asset_repurpose` widens the *fiction/look* vocabulary of identity; it does **not** author *mechanical* identity (the mechanic being the fantasy). A statue-as-enemy is a re-skin of an existing enemy verb, not a new verb. Deep, mechanical cohesion is bought at the pillar-feasibility gate (§7.3), not here. Identity is gated downstream by fantasy-legibility + through-line (§7), read by persona/human, never asserted by the tool.

## 4. LOOK / MOOD AUTHORING — the primary lever a real art director pulls

Draft 1's only lighting lever was "add skylight + directional at 35°" and its only lighting metric was luminance. That is exposure, not *look*. **80% of "is this beautiful" is the post/grade/atmosphere pass, and it is also what unifies mixed packs into one world.**

**`look_author`** (bridge op → configures a `APostProcessVolume` + `ExponentialHeightFog` + `SkyAtmosphere`/`SkyLight`) — an authorable **mood**: color grade (LUT + white balance + saturation/contrast/tint), bloom, exposure (manual/auto with clamps), vignette, film grain, fog density/color/height, and (where enabled) a GI/AO nudge. The Art Director drives it from the **creative pillar + art bible** (§7.3): "cold overcast, teal-orange, low bloom, heavy fog" is now a *setting*, not a wish. This is the tool behind the `distinctiveness/identity`, `art-direction coherence`, and `lighting quality` dimensions — and a primary lever for the **skin-deep** layer of the through-line read (a shared grade is what makes a mixed pack read as one world, §7).

**`style_cohesion`** — the #1 asset-flip tell is style clash (realistic PBR next to low-poly stylized), and multi-pack agent games *will* mix (Aesir mixed MC_Turrets + EasyGameUI + Sci_Fi + a hub). **Two variants, correctly phased:**
- **P0 pixel variant (`internal/stylecohesion`, no tags):** operate purely on **captured-frame statistics** — palette clustering, luminance/contrast distribution, edge-density/shading-model heuristics (flat-shaded low-poly vs textured PBR read very differently in these stats). Hard-flag a frame whose visible regions split into two incompatible style clusters above a coverage threshold. Ships and self-validates in Phase 0 with **zero dependency on `asset_tag`/`material_introspect`.**
- **P1 tag upgrade:** once `asset_tag` style labels + the `material_introspect` style signal exist, cross-check per-actor tagged styles against the pixel read for a higher-precision flag.

A shared post/LUT pass via `look_author` is the sanctioned unifier when a mix is unavoidable.

**"Establish a look" is an explicit `dress-scene` step** (§9): compose space → light → **grade/mood** → critique. A scene without an authored look is incomplete, the same way a scene with a cube is.

## 5. SPATIAL / LEVEL DESIGN — design, not set-dressing

RC5 is diagnosed correctly, but `kit_compose` + `scatter_designed` is still *bottom-up dressing*. §3.5 ships the **material**; this section ships the **intent**.

**`spatial_compose`** (bridge op + Go reasoning over the scene) — a top-down spatial-design layer:
- **Critical path + landmark/vista authoring:** place a readable **landmark silhouette** (the tower, the crashed ship) as a focal anchor, lay a **critical path** from PlayerStart to objective, and use **leading lines** (kit walls, light, terrain) toward the landmark. Makes `composition/focal-hierarchy` scoreable *and fixable*.
- **Combat-space reasoning (shooters/defense):** compute **cover** (mesh occluders along sightlines) and **sightlines** between spawn points, cover, and objective; flag "no cover on the approach" or "spawn has a clean line to the objective" — the layout Aesir's thin box arena never had. **This is also where a spatial scaffold axis (e.g. engagement range / path length) becomes a *controlled* property** — see §6.1b: a scaffold axis is only non-degenerate-by-construction if the state that flips its optimum is state the scaffold controls, and combat-space geometry is exactly such state when `spatial_compose` sets it deliberately.
- **Encounter pacing over space:** sequence encounter beats along the critical path (breather → pressure → spike → payoff), consumed by `encounter_author` (§6.4), with the **payoff beat often realized as a `setpiece` (§6.4).**
- **Objective readability:** `objective_place` + a check that the objective is visible/framed from the critical path.

**`camera_frame`** (bridge op) — set the *capture/screenshot* camera to a composed shot (rule-of-thirds on the landmark, horizon placement) so composition is authorable and the critic scores an intended frame. (Distinct from `camera_rig` (§6.4), which authors the *gameplay* camera's motion/feel.)

**`design_review` gains spatial dimensions** (§7): **spatial flow**, **objective readability**, and **pacing** — scored over a playthrough, not prettiness. A flat evenly-lit grid of well-scattered props now *fails* the spatial read even if it passes every floor audit.

## 6. GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS + DESIGN QUALITY + FEEL + AUDIO + GENERATION — the loop, the *fun*, the verb, the juice, the sound, and the search past the box

RC4/RC8/RC9/RC10/RC12. We do **not** pretend an LLM can reliably author arbitrary K2 graphs. We solve it with **vetted, parameter-driven components the agent composes** + **narrow BP-graph glue**. Libraries and their gates: systems, **design-quality**, **verb-feel + juice + input-inject**, **audio**, **theming**, and the **generative measured search** that reaches past the scaffold box *in variety*.

### 6.1 System components (the loop) — with a headless-simulability BUILD RULE

Ship a small runtime module (`MCPGameplay`) of tested, parameter-driven `UActorComponent`s/actors, instantiated with the *existing* `blueprint_add_component` + `blueprint_set_defaults` — no new authoring surface:
- `USpawnerComponent` — spawn N of class X at tagged points over T seconds, budget/cap.
- `UWaveDirectorComponent` — escalating waves, archetype gates, boss cadence, intermission (Aesir's `StartNextWave`, generalized + data-driven).
- `UEconomyComponent` — tick currency, income/upkeep, costs, affordability (PolyWorld's `Step()`, generalized — and actually *wired*, unlike `PolyCatalog`).
- `UHealthComponent`, `UInteractionComponent` (trigger→event), `UObjectiveComponent` (defend N waves / destroy all nests / survive T / reach X).
- `UAIBrainComponent` — a small BT-backed pursue/attack/flee/patrol brain.

**BUILD RULE (load-bearing — makes `balance_sweep` off-lease by construction; fixes the CTO's economics blocker).** Ground truth: the runtime director is world-bound — `APolyDirector : public AActor` (`PolyDirector.h:42`), and a `UEconomyComponent` needs `GetOwner()->GetWorld()` — so sweeping *its* logic requires a live world = the scarce **editor lease** (§8). But PolyWorld already contains the counter-pattern: `PolyCatalog.h` exposes an **engine-free struct + free function** — `FPolyResolvedCatalog` + `POLYWORLD_API bool BuildResolvedCatalog(const UPolyEconomyConfig*, FPolyResolvedCatalog&, FString&)` (`PolyCatalog.h:29,46`) — referenced only by `PolyCatalogTest.cpp`, i.e. a headless-simulable economy that was simply never wired or swept. So the rule for **every** §6.1 component whose logic we want to sweep for free:

> Factor the component's simulation into a **pure, engine-free, seedable struct + free step function** (mirroring `FPolyResolvedCatalog`/`BuildResolvedCatalog`), and make the `UActorComponent` a thin wrapper that ticks it. Then `balance_sweep` runs the struct headless — no editor, no world, massively parallel. A component that does *not* factor this way is *not* off-lease: its sweep runs through `playtest_capture` simulate mode and **competes for the editor lease** (priced accordingly in §8).

The lesson from dead `PolyCatalog`: the hard part is *wiring it in* — and its struct/free-function shape is exactly the shape that makes headless sweeping *and the generative search* (§6.5) free. We adopt that shape as the standard, not the exception.

### 6.1b Design-quality tooling (RC10 — the co-master miss) — *decisions baked into structure, and the keystone scaffold designed, not asserted*

A loop that runs is not a game. This subsection makes **design quality authored, simulated, and gated** — and, critically, keeps the agent's **fix-space matched to the defect-space** (§2.2) by baking decisions into *structure* rather than asking an LLM to invent mechanics it cannot author (RC4). **The scaffold's non-degenerate axes are not *asserted* — one reference scaffold is *designed and proven* (Phase 0.6), and a full-sweep non-degeneracy gate — with a satisfiable fenced fallback (fix 2) — attaches to every scaffold that ships.**

**Decisions are baked into scaffolds, not invented (fixes the CTO's §2.2-coverage blocker).** A decision-free loop is usually decision-free *structurally* — PolyWorld has literally one meaningful verb. If the critic can name "decision-free loop" but the agent's only lever is retuning numbers, that is the exact spin/stop-naming failure §2.2 forbids. Resolution:
- **`loop_scaffold` genre templates ship structurally decision-rich skeletons** — each genre skeleton bakes in **≥2 orthogonal meaningful axes**. The decisions pre-exist in tested C++/data; the agent instantiates and tunes them.
- **The agent-facing design gate is "instantiate a decision-bearing scaffold + tune it to non-degenerate,"** *not* "invent meaningful decisions." Tuning (datatable/param) can make a baked decision *live* or *dead*; that is squarely in the agent's authority.
- **"Add a new mechanic to a structurally decision-poor loop" is an explicit human-in-loop escalation** — *or* a candidate the generative search proposes and a human ratifies (§6.5). A net-new *scaffold axis* is a C++ change; the design critic surfaces the need rather than scoring the agent against a fix-space it doesn't have.

**The keystone must be designed, not asserted (fixes both reviewers' loop_scaffold blocker).** Every design guarantee routes through "the scaffold ships ≥2 orthogonal non-degenerate axes." Draft 4 asserted that property and never built or proved a single scaffold. This plan:
- **Phase 0.6 designs and proves ONE reference scaffold** (the two-axis economy, below) whose axes are non-degenerate across its swept tuning grid — this reference is the *template* the library is built from, not a hypothetical.
- **Every shipped scaffold carries a full-sweep non-degeneracy acceptance gate (below), with a fenced-corner fallback for satisfiability.**
- **Scaffold authoring is senior-design labor, quantitatively costed in §8** as a named workstream peer to the calibration corpora — because designing an axis whose optimum genuinely shifts with controllable state is exactly the scarce, expensive skill agent games lack.

**The full-sweep non-degeneracy gate (attaches to *every* scaffold that ships), and its fenced fallback (fix 2).** It is not enough that the seed tuning is non-degenerate; a scaffold's axes must stay live *across the grid* an agent might tune into. The gate, and its honest satisfiability escape hatch:

> **Ideal gate:** run `balance_sweep` over the **full declared tuning grid** of the scaffold. At **every** grid point, the **optimal action must shift with game-state** (the axis is *live*), not collapse to one dominant choice. A scaffold where any axis becomes state-independently dominant *anywhere* in its grid fails the ideal gate.
> **Satisfiability risk (named).** Demanding **100%** grid-liveness may be **unsatisfiable** for some genres — a real two-axis design often has *corners* of its tuning space where one axis collapses (e.g. AoE at zero splash-radius is just a weak single-target). Requiring perfection would reject usable scaffolds and stall the library.
> **Fenced fallback (the shipped rule):** a scaffold **passes** if its axes stay live across **≥85%** of the swept grid volume **AND** the collapse-corners are **FENCED** — the tuning sub-ranges where an axis collapses are marked *invalid* and `loop_scaffold`/`datatable_*` **clamp the agent's tunable ranges so it literally cannot enter them** (a validated param-range constraint enforced at tuning time, mirroring UE's `ClampMin`/`ClampMax` metadata). An agent tuning a fenced scaffold therefore *cannot* ship the PolyWorld metronome, because the metronome corner is unreachable. A scaffold that cannot reach ≥85% *even with fencing* is rejected and re-designed (senior labor, §8). Only fenced-or-perfect scaffolds ship.

**The orthogonality-by-construction principle, and the fixed flagship example (fixes the degenerate-example blocker).** An axis is non-degenerate-by-construction **only if the state that flips its optimum is state the scaffold controls** — not emergent geometry the scaffold doesn't author. Draft 4's flagship *turret range-vs-firerate* fails this: range only matters if enemies spend time at long engagement distance, which depends on **map path-length/geometry** — a spatial property `loop_scaffold` doesn't control — so in practice fire-rate dominates and the "decision" is fake. Replaced with a genuinely orthogonal pair:
- **Wave-defense reference axes: *single-target-vs-AoE damage investment* × *economy expand-vs-bank timing*.**
  - **Single-target vs AoE:** single-target (high per-hit, no splash) wins vs **elite/armored/boss** waves (few high-HP targets); AoE (splash, lower per-target) wins vs **dense swarms** (many low-HP targets). The flipping state is **wave composition** — which the `UWaveDirectorComponent` + `encounter_author` schedule *controls*. Orthogonal by construction. (The collapse-corner to fence: AoE with splash-radius clamped near zero degenerates to single-target — so splash-radius has a fenced `ClampMin`.)
  - **Expand-vs-bank:** expand-now (buy production capacity) wins if you can amortize before the next spike; bank (hold liquidity) wins right before a **spike wave**. The flipping state is the **spike schedule** — also wave-director-controlled.
  - Both optima flip with **controlled** state, so the pair passes the full-sweep gate (with two small fenced corners) and *stays* live across the grid — and the wave-composition variety that keeps the first axis live *is* novelty (§6.1b novelty), tying the axes to the arc.
- **If a scaffold genuinely needs a spatial axis** (e.g. an engagement-range axis), the spatial dependency is **made explicit and controlled** via `spatial_compose` (§5): the scaffold declares a critical-path length / cover layout as a *scaffold parameter*, so the state that flips the axis is authored, not emergent. A spatial axis is only allowed if its flipping-state is controlled.

**The interesting-decision spec (authoring, required).** Every loop the Systems Designer ships must declare, as data, its **intended meaningful decisions** drawn from the scaffold's baked axes: for each, the choice, the situations where each branch is correct, and the *tell* that should push a player one way. Example (Aesir, fixed): *"Invest in single-target vs AoE — single-target wins the elite waves (N,M), AoE wins the swarm waves (K,L); the tell is the announced next-wave composition."* Example (PolyWorld, the one it lacks): *"Expand economy now vs bank for the wave-N spike."* This spec is the ground truth the audits below check against, **it is the axis set the policy generator searches over**, and **it is the seed the generative search mutates (§6.5)** — a declared decision that no simulation ever makes matter is a fake choice, and the ship-gate flags it.

**`decision_audit`** (deterministic, `internal/decisionaudit`) — **scoped to exactly what a `(state, available_actions, chosen_action)` trace yields — nothing more:**
- **Decision density** — count of meaningfully-*distinct viable* actions available per minute (from the `available_actions` field of the trace, not "how many buttons exist").
- **Cadence entropy** — is the *observed* action sequence periodic/uniform (low entropy = a metronome = boring) or state-responsive (varied)? PolyWorld's "uniform cadence" is exactly low cadence-entropy; **this is catchable at the floor, in Phase 0, deterministically — the strongest form of the fix.**
- **Observed action-frequency skew** — is one action taken in a dominating share of decision points? A *diagnostic* signal, explicitly **not** a proof of dominance — proof requires counterfactuals the trace does not contain. `decision_audit` **does not claim "EV-optimal in all states."** That claim lives in `balance_sweep`, which can actually search.

**`balance_sweep`** (Go orchestrator over headless sim — RC11's balance half; **the home of dominance detection, with a named policy generator**):
- **Substrate (no hand-wave):** for §6.1-BUILD-RULE-factored loops (`FPolyResolvedCatalog`-shaped structs), the sim runs as a **poolable, engine-free C++ harness — no editor at all, off-lease, massively parallel.** For loops not so factored, it runs through `playtest_capture` **simulate mode** + `time_dilation` (`slomo`) — **editor-lease-bound, priced as such (§8).** Metrics extracted with `playtest_evaluate` + a small stats reducer.
- **The policy generator, named:**
  - **Pure-struct economy loops:** **exhaustive / grid search over the interesting-decision-spec's declared axes** (expand-rate × bank-threshold × build-order).
  - **AI / action loops:** a **small set of scripted heuristic policies derived from the spec's declared branches** (e.g. *single-target-priority* vs *AoE-priority* vs *mixed*), run headless.
  - **Honest caveat, stated in the output:** this covers the **declared** decision space, **not emergent** strategies a human might discover. `balance_sweep` proves "no declared option strictly dominates and outcomes swing across the declared policy set"; it does not prove global game-theoretic balance. The persona (§7.4b) and the human milestone (§8b) check emergent/undeclared degeneracy.
- **What it reports:** **win-rate curve** vs difficulty tier; **TTK / survival-time distribution**; **resource-curve stability** (snowball vs stall); **dominant-policy flag** (*does one policy win across the swept set*); **degenerate-optimum detection** (is the best policy a single repeated action — the PolyWorld failure — or does the optimum shift with state); and — for the full-sweep gate above — **per-grid-point axis-liveness** (feeding both the ≥85% coverage measure and the fenced-corner set).

**`novelty_audit`** (deterministic, `internal/decisionaudit` — RC10b, the whole-session arc). Point-in-time design checks miss that "good" over a session is a *variety* property. `novelty_audit` reads a full-session trace + the authored **new-element schedule** and computes a **novelty budget**:
- **New-element cadence** — the sequence of *first appearances* over the session of a distinct (enemy archetype | spawn configuration | modifier | objective type | setpiece beat | mechanic-toggle), observed via `counts.<class>` first-nonzero and `gamestate.*` transitions.
- **Floor failure:** a window longer than a genre-tuned threshold (e.g. > N minutes / > M waves) with **zero first-appearances** is flagged — the whole-session form of the PolyWorld defect ("nothing new after 90 seconds"), made deterministic.
- **Fix-space (matched to defect-space, per §2.2):** novelty is satisfied by **recombination of the scaffold's baked structural axes** — a new enemy archetype, spawn config, modifier, objective, or setpiece reveal — **all instanced from the `loop_scaffold` library, none requiring net-new C++.** A net-new *mechanic* beyond the scaffold's axes is the same human-in-loop escalation (or a `design_explore` proposal, §6.5).

**Progression / escalation read** (`balance_sweep` mode) — does **difficulty grow AND player capability grow** together, or does one flatline? Flags the treadmill (enemy HP scales, player options don't) and the walkover (player snowballs, enemies don't).

**The `design_quality` critic dimension (§7) gates on all of these**, and it holds **veto** (§9). You cannot score design ≥ bar while `decision_audit` shows a metronome, `balance_sweep` shows a degenerate optimum or dominant policy, or `novelty_audit` shows a dead stretch. **`novelty` is its own veto sub-dimension**, symmetric to decision-density. **But note the honest limit:** this whole stack is a **proxy** for the blind-human north-star, and until §7.5's proxy-validation milestone confirms it correlates, it is labeled an **unvalidated proxy** — a label that also constrains what `design_explore` may *claim* (§6.5, fix 3).

### 6.2 Verb-feel + juice + in-motion + INPUT-INJECT components (RC8) — *feel-quality attempted in the loop, honestly hedged*

Feel is two layers (RC8). Draft 3 tooled the top (juice) and missed the bottom (the verb); draft 4 tooled the verb but left feel-*quality* out of the loop because the persona could not experience it. This draft adds a path to put it in the loop — **but names it a *hoped-for* path, not an expected one** (fix 5). **Every juice component *instances* an existing pack Niagara/Cascade system + sound at runtime — it does not author graphs — so it stays inside the stated non-goal** (§12).

**The core-verb / control-feel surface (the biggest single feel lever).** A first-class, parameterized tuning surface for *the verb itself*, with genre presets in `loop_scaffold` **expected to be tuned, not shipped as defaults:**
- **`UVerbFeelComponent` (movement):** accel / decel / air-control / friction / ground-vs-air control / **coyote-time** / **jump-buffer** / variable-jump-height / max-speed shaping. Turns Aesir's raw `MaxWalkSpeed=400`+`RotationRate=540°/s` (`EnemyCharacter.cpp:45-47`) from magic numbers into an authored, iterable feel.
- **Weapon-verb tuning (on the weapon component):** fire-cadence feel, **recoil-recovery curve**, aim-assist cone / hit-window / registration feel, weapon "weight" (windup/settle).
- **Genre presets in `loop_scaffold`:** each genre skeleton ships a *starting* verb-feel preset flagged **"tune me."** A good shooter and a bad shooter differ almost entirely in this tuning.

Juice components (instanced, not authored):
- `UImpactFXComponent` — pack VFX + sound at a hit/impact location (muzzle flash, spark, dust, tracer beam).
- `UCameraShakeComponent` — `UCameraShakeBase` on fire/hit/explosion (kick, not drift).
- `UHitStopComponent` — brief global time-dilation (2–4 frame freeze) on heavy impacts.
- `URecoilComponent` — additive weapon/camera recoil + recovery curve.
- `UDamageNumberComponent` — floating damage numbers / hit confirmation.
- `UHitReactComponent` — hit-react montage / flash / knockback on the target.
- `ULocomotionComponent` *(the "dead in motion" fix)* — blend a pack's idle/walk/run to ground speed (C++ blend over instanced sequences, **not** an authored AnimBP) and clamp orient-to-movement snap. Directly fixes `EnemyCharacter.cpp`'s raw `PlayAnimation` foot-slide + 540°/s snap.

**`input_inject` — the runtime native hot op, a prioritized Phase-3B deliverable (opens a *hoped-for* path to in-loop feel-quality).** Ground truth: the current server's `input_action`/`input_axis` are **offline `DefaultInput.ini` mapping ops** (`affordances.go:38-39`, `Offline: true`) — *not* runtime input injection. So the persona (§7.4b) drives only **semantic `BlueprintCallable` verbs**, which means it acts *above* the frame loop and **structurally cannot experience sub-100ms feel.** That leaves `verb_response` certifying only "prompt, non-degenerate, within budget" — **correct-but-mushy.** We promote the op:
- **`input_inject`** (plugin: `MCPCapture`/`MCPControlSubsystem`, native hot op) — inject **frame-level input** (key/axis/mouse state at the `UPlayerInput`/`InputComponent` layer) into a live PIE session, so an agent can *press and hold and release* on real frames, not just call verbs. **Prioritized in Phase 3B**, spec'd as an engine-integration op (route through `APlayerController`/`UPlayerInput::InputKey`/axis with a documented fallback of a synthetic `UInputComponent` binding driver).
- **What it *could* unlock (hoped-for, not assumed):** the persona drives the *verb* at frame resolution, and — **IF and only if the feel-quality persona bet clears its separate, hardest calibration gate (§7.4c)** — carries an in-loop feel-quality gradient. `input_inject` is the *mechanical precondition* for that read; it is **not** sufficient for it. An LLM watching a 60fps filmstrip of its own injected inputs still cannot proprioceptively judge weight/snap/juice-landing; whether it can *learn* to predict human *felt* labels is an open research question (§7.4c), and the most-likely-to-never-clear one in the plan.
- **The default expectation is the human feel-pulse.** Until — and on every genre where — the feel-quality persona bet has *not* cleared, feel-quality is **fundamentally human-gated** via the periodic human feel-pulse (§8b), which is a **named PERMANENT fallback**, not a temporary bridge. We do not let a filmstrip-watching persona pretend to judge game feel, and we do not present the `input_inject`+persona path as the expected outcome. It is a bet we fund and hope for.

**The event-detection contract — the actual hard part, specified (two-tier).** `feel_audit`/`audio_audit`/`verb_response` are defined "per fire/hit/death event," and *detecting those events in an uninstrumented build is the real problem.*
- **Tier 1 — timeline heuristic floor (zero game instrumentation, ships in Phase 0):** **`fire`** = an input/exec action coincident with a trace or projectile-actor spawn (`world_query line_trace` / a `counts.<Projectile>` increment). **`hit`** = a tracked-actor HP-path decrease or a damage log line. **`death`** = an actor destroy / `counts.<class>` decrement (`pie_observe`'s `counts.EnemyCharacter`, `rubric.go:428`). Needs no `MCPGameplay` components — so Phase 0's self-validation is legitimate.
- **Tier 2 — instrumented high-fidelity (Phase 3B):** `MCPGameplay` components emit a **standardized structured event log** (`{t, kind, instigator, location}`) that *supersedes* the heuristic where present.

**`feel_audit`** (deterministic, `internal/feelaudit`) — measures **feedback presence**: for each detected event, assert a **visual + audio + camera** response fires within **N ms** (default 120ms), plus the **input→visible-response latency** number. Aesir's `DrawDebugLine` turret **fails deterministically**, exactly like `primitive_audit` fails the cube.

**`verb_response`** (deterministic, `internal/feelaudit`) — a **verb-response fingerprint**: from the 60fps burst, extract the **input→state-delta curve shape** (position/velocity for movement; damage/recoil/aim for a weapon). Compare against the genre preset's expected envelope and flag a **degenerate response** (instant teleport-snap, dead zone, no settle). CI-gates *presence + non-degeneracy*; **verb-QUALITY is the human feel-pulse by default, and the persona-in-loop gradient only on genres where the feel-quality bet clears its separate gate (§7.4c)** — never assumed.

> **Presence, not quality — scope stated honestly.** `feel_audit`/`verb_response` prove feedback and response are *present, prompt, and non-degenerate*; they do **not** prove the feel is *good*. So: (a) **over-juice ceilings** as hard CI numbers (shake magnitude/frequency budget, hit-stop cap, per-event FX-count cap); (b) **input→response latency** + the **verb-response curve envelope** as hard CI numbers; (c) **feel-QUALITY / verb-feel-QUALITY is the periodic human feel-pulse (§8b) — a PERMANENT fallback — and enters the loop as a persona gradient ONLY where the separate, hardest feel-quality calibration bet clears (§7.4c), never a semantic-verb-LLM guess and never assumed.** A 60fps filmstrip burst does not certify game feel, and we no longer claim it does — nor that `input_inject` alone changes that.

**In-motion audit** (deterministic, over the retarget/locomotion filmstrip) — compare per-frame root translation vs animation playback to score **foot-sliding**, and flag **snap-rotation** above a deg/frame threshold. CI catch for the `EnemyCharacter` motion defect; acceptance gate for `retarget_setup` + `ULocomotionComponent`.

### 6.3 Audio components + the capture SPIKE (RC9)

A **first-class** audio library in `MCPGameplay` (grep confirms zero audio substrate to inherit):
- `UAudioEventComponent` — trigger `USoundBase`/SoundCues on gameplay events (fire/hit/death/UI/objective) via `PlaySoundAtLocation`/`UAudioComponent`, sourced from the pack SFX index (`pack_orient`).
- A **music/ambience layer** — looping ambience + a simple state-driven music switch (calm/combat/victory).

`design_review` gains a **listening pass** over the track (presence, mix balance, is-it-annoying-on-loop, identity).

### 6.4 Encounter + setpiece + camera + theming + glue authoring

**`encounter_author`** (bridge op → DataAsset for `UWaveDirectorComponent`) — archetypes, tagged points, escalation, **pacing beats over the space (§5)**, win/lose as **data**, and a **new-element-introduction schedule** (the authored input `novelty_audit` checks against). Feeds `design_review`'s pacing critique, `balance_sweep`'s escalation read, and `novelty_audit`.

**`setpiece_author`** (bridge op → a lightweight sequence DataAsset played by a `USetpieceComponent`) *(memorable-moment, with a craft floor)* — a **timeline that sequences camera moves + spawns + audio stings + a reveal/vista** at a point on the critical path, driven by the pillar. A difficulty spike is *not* a memorable moment; a memorable moment is *authored*. Two-part evaluation:
- **Deterministic CRAFT floor (Layer A):** assert the *ingredients of a beat* — a **quiet/buildup** window before the reveal, a **contrast delta** (a measurable jump in luminance / audio RMS / enemy-density vs the preceding window), an **aligned audio sting**, and a **camera emphasis.** A flat "moment" that is just more-of-the-same **fails the craft floor.**
- **Experiential scoring moves OFF the filmstrip:** whether the beat *surprised and landed* is scored by the **persona/human read** (§7.4b/§8b) — and feeds the **delight/surprise** axis (§7.4b) and the **through-line** read (§7), never an LLM grading a replay. The craft floor certifies the moment was *built*; the persona/human certifies it *worked* and *fit*.

**`theme_author`** (bridge op → naming/flavor DataAsset + framing hooks) *(RC12, the SKIN-DEEP fiction between pillar and pixels)* — turns the pillar's *fantasy* into the texture of play at the fiction/framing layer: **objective/enemy/UI naming + flavor text + framing** ("the last lighthouse keeper" → objectives named for the keep, enemies framed as the encroaching dark, UI voice in-fiction). Cheap, data-only, and it separates "generic wave-defense in a nice LUT" from a *legible* identity. Feeds **fantasy-legibility** and — with `asset_repurpose` (§3.6) and `look_author` — the **skin-deep** layer of the through-line read (§7). **Honest limit:** `theme_author` skins the fantasy onto the mechanic; it does **not** make the mechanic *be* the fantasy. That deeper cohesion is bought at the pillar-feasibility gate (§7.3), not here.

**`camera_rig`** (bridge op → configures a gameplay camera component: spring-arm boom, follow lag, FOV, aim-offset, FOV-kick on fire/sprint) *(the static-camera half of "dead in motion")* — authors the *gameplay* camera's motion and feel, with a **camera-feel check.** Distinct from `camera_frame` (§5).

**`ai_setup` / `behavior_tree_author`** (plugin: `MCPAuthoring`) — create BT + Blackboard + AIController + nav-link with common tasks (patrol/chase/attack-in-range/flee).

**`loop_scaffold`** (Go orchestrator) — opinionated genre scaffolds (wave-defense, tycoon, arena-shooter, collectathon) that wire GameMode + system components + **verb-feel presets ("tune me") + juice + audio + locomotion + camera_rig** + win/lose + HUD + a **full-sweep-proven-or-fenced structurally decision-rich skeleton (≥2 orthogonal axes, §6.1b) + starter interesting-decision spec + starter new-element schedule + a `theme_author` stub** in **one verified transaction**, then run `playtest_capture` + `balance_sweep` (including the **full-sweep non-degeneracy gate + fenced-corner clamps**). The agent starts from a playable, *juicy, audible, decision-bearing, theme-able* skeleton — not a blank `AGameMode`. **This is where "bake decisions into structure," "novelty via recombination," "verb-feel presets," and "fenced tuning ranges" land — and where `design_explore` (§6.5) plugs in to search past the seed.** Every scaffold in the library is a *designed, proven* artifact (senior-design labor, §8), templated from the Phase-0.6 reference.

**`bp_add_event` / `bp_add_node` / `bp_wire` / `bp_add_variable` / `bp_add_function`** (plugin: `MCPAuthoring`, K2 + `FBlueprintEditorUtils`) — scoped to **"wire events to component calls"** (`OnHit → ImpactFX.Play + Audio.Play + HitStop.Trigger`), not arbitrary logic. Every graph edit gated by **compile + playtest.**

### 6.5 The generative surface — design as a MEASURED SEARCH, specified as MAP-Elites *(fixes the generative-stub / one-resolution-shallower blocker)*

The plan so far is a superb *judge* and — in draft 5 — a *sketched* generator: it vetted proposals at full depth (reusing the audits) but *proposed* thinly and costed its fuel qualitatively. This section brings `internal/explore` to `internal/decisionaudit` build-spec depth: a **named quality-diversity algorithm (MAP-Elites)** with an explicit descriptor space, a stated archive resolution, a concrete regime-mutation generator, a falsifiable unit test, a numeric K, and quantified fuel. Generation is a search **with a fitness function and a diversity metric**, symmetric to the measurement engine.

**`design_explore`** (Go orchestrator, `internal/explore`). Algorithm: **MAP-Elites** (illumination search). It maintains an **archive** of the best-found candidate per *behavior cell*, runs **variation → evaluation → archive-insertion** for a budget of E evaluations, and returns the archive's top elites. Concretely:

- **Genotype (what a candidate *is*) = the scaffold's declared axis-parameter vector.** For the wave-defense reference: `{ active_axes[], single_target_vs_aoe_split, splash_radius (fenced), expand_rate, bank_threshold, build_order, wave_composition_schedule[], spike_schedule[], asset_role_map{} }`. Every gene is a value the agent already has authority to set (datatable/param/schedule/asset-role) — **nothing in the genotype is net-new C++** (RC4). A genotype that violates a fenced-corner clamp (§6.1b) is **projected back into the valid range before evaluation** (never allowed to enter a collapse corner).

- **Behavior-descriptor (BD) space — the diversity axes, named and binned (this is the resolution draft 5 lacked):** a 3-D descriptor computed from the measurement engine's own outputs:
  1. **decision-cadence-entropy bin** — from `decision_audit`'s cadence entropy — **5 bins** (metronome → highly state-responsive).
  2. **win-rate-slope bin** — the slope of `balance_sweep`'s win-rate curve vs difficulty tier (how sharply difficulty bites) — **5 bins** (flat/walkover → steep/brutal).
  3. **novelty-profile bin** — from `novelty_audit`'s new-element cadence (front-loaded → evenly-paced → back-loaded reveals) — **4 bins**.
  - **Archive resolution: 5 × 5 × 4 = 100 cells.** Each cell stores at most one elite — the highest-**fitness** feasible genotype whose behavior falls in that cell. Filling distinct cells *is* variety: two elites in different cells are behaviorally distinct *by construction of the descriptor*.

- **Fitness (a scalar, only among the feasible):** feasibility is a **hard filter** — a candidate is discarded (never inserted) unless `balance_sweep` shows it passes the full-sweep gate (≥85% axis-liveness, no unfenced collapse) AND `decision_audit`/`novelty_audit` clear their floors. Among feasible candidates competing for a cell, **fitness = balance-margin** (distance from the nearest dominance/collapse boundary — "how robustly non-degenerate") **+ persona-delight where the persona is calibrated** (§7.4b), else balance-margin alone. Higher fitness evicts the incumbent of that cell.

- **Variation operators (the PROPOSE half — three families, each a concrete mutation on the genotype):**
  1. **Axis recombination / cross-scaffold transplant — the regime-mutation generator (spec'd concretely).** Given scaffold A's axis-parameter vector and a *donor* axis proven non-degenerate in another shipped scaffold B (e.g. tycoon's *risk-vs-safety spend*), the generator: (i) appends B's axis genes to A's `active_axes[]`; (ii) **re-binds B's flipping-state hook onto one of A's controlled-state channels** — B's axis flipped on B's spike schedule, so it is wired to A's `spike_schedule[]`/`wave_composition_schedule[]` (the only state A controls); (iii) re-runs the full-sweep gate on the *combined* grid and fences any new collapse corner. This is the operator that makes the search reach the *edges* of the authored design space. **It is fuel-gated (below).**
  2. **Within-scaffold regime mutation** — Gaussian/regime perturbation of `wave_composition_schedule[]` / `spike_schedule[]` / the split parameters *within the fenced grid* (a new composition cadence that flips the single-target/AoE optimum on a novel rhythm; a new expand/bank spike pattern). Recombination of authored parts, never net-new C++.
  3. **Asset re-purposing (§3.6)** — a discrete mutation of `asset_role_map{}` toward the pillar (prop-as-weapon, statue-as-enemy, on-palette recolor). Changes *skin-deep identity*, not the verb.

- **Falsifiable unit test (symmetric to `decision_audit` "must fail on current PolyWorld").** Construct a **synthetic two-axis scaffold with a KNOWN second viable regime planted off-seed** (a distinct, feasible, high-fitness genotype occupying a BD cell ≥ 2 bins from the seed's cell in ≥1 descriptor). Seed the archive with only the seed genotype and run `design_explore` for E evaluations. **Assert:** the search **rediscovers the planted regime** — populates that off-seed cell with a feasible elite — within E. **If it cannot rediscover a planted regime, the search is broken** (its variation is too weak or its feasibility filter too tight). This is a hard, green/red CI test, not an aspiration.

- **Numeric K (what it surfaces).** After the budget, `design_explore` returns the **top K = 8 feasible elites**, each from a **distinct BD cell** (guaranteed behaviorally distinct), ranked by fitness, with their full genotype + `balance_sweep`/`novelty_audit`/persona reports attached. K=8 is the batch a human designer can meaningfully triage in one review; it is a config, not a magic constant.

- **Fuel — quantified, and tied to the scaffold-library cost (fix 2).** Operator 1 (cross-transplant) has **nothing to transplant FROM** until the senior-authored scaffold library has **≥ 2 proven donor scaffolds** beyond the target. So `design_explore`'s power is **strictly gated on scaffold-library fuel** (§8): with only the Phase-0.6 reference (library size **L=1**), operators 2 and 3 are live but operator 1 is inert; operator 1's reach grows only as the **quantitatively-costed** senior-design workstream (§8, workstream 3) ships more full-sweep-proven scaffolds. **The search cannot outrun its fuel, and its fuel is expensive senior-design labor — stated, not hidden.**

**Where its power is bounded (honest, per the calibration discipline):**
- **The headless half (balance_sweep / decision_audit / novelty_audit) is trustworthy from Phase 3A** for BUILD-RULE-factored struct economies — so the search is strongest exactly where the design ceiling is strongest (systems/decision-driven genres, §11 scope). **But (fix 3):** those instruments are an **unvalidated proxy** until §7.5's proxy-validation milestone. The BD descriptors and the fitness scalar are proxy-derived, so **until proxies validate, `design_explore`'s "distinct" and "better" are proxy-claims only** — it surfaces **balance-vetted-but-taste-UNVETTED** candidates for human judgment (see phasing).
- **The persona half only vets where the persona is calibrated (§7.4c).** On genres where it isn't, `design_explore` returns balance-vetted-but-taste-unvetted candidates for a human to judge — never a claim of "found a fun one."
- **It reaches toward VARIETY, not ORIGINALITY (fix 6, said plainly).** The archive spreads across the *edges of the authored design space* — the union of all scaffold axes. It **cannot** produce a **new verb** (a mechanic outside that union); that is net-new C++ (RC4), a human-in-loop scaffold change the search can only *motivate* by surfacing "every elite wants a lever we don't have." "Reach toward original" is retired from the plan's vocabulary; "reach toward variety" is the honest claim.
- **Surface, don't auto-ship:** survivors are **surfaced to the Systems/Balance Designer + a human** (the through-line and pillar-fit read, §7, is human/persona, and a varied combination still needs a human's taste to bless). `design_explore` widens the reachable set and pre-vets it; **it does not autonomously ship an untasted game.**

**Phasing (fix 3 — proxy-validation BEFORE the reach claim).** `design_explore` lands in **Phase 5**, after `balance_sweep`+`novelty_audit` (Phase 3A) and the calibrated persona (Phase 4) exist. **Within Phase 5, ordering is explicit:** (1) the **proxy-validation milestone** (§7.5) correlates the design-proxy stack against the blind-human north-star; (2) **only after it clears** may `design_explore`'s reach metric claim its candidates are "meaningfully different AND at least one is *better*" — because "different" and "better" are read through the (now-validated) proxy. **Before proxy-validation, `design_explore` runs but its output is downgraded to "≥K viable, proxy-distinct candidates surfaced for HUMAN taste ratification"** — the exact discipline applied to the uncalibrated persona (§7.4c). Its metric is a **reach metric**: on the reference scaffold, the search surfaces ≥K viable-and-behaviorally-distinct candidates that a human designer judges "meaningfully different from the seed, and at least one is *better*" — the falsifiable proof the plan generates *variety* past the box, claimed only once the proxy that measures "better" has earned it.

## 7. THE TASTE + CRITIQUE LOOP — calibrated to tell good from mediocre, in look, in play, *and as one whole game*

The centerpiece and the direct fix for RC1. What is missing is a **judge** that takes images/playthroughs/audio/play-traces and returns a *quality* verdict with **specific, actionable defects**, scored against a reference — and a judge of the **whole-game through-line** above the per-axis scores. **It ships in Phase 4, after every fix tool (§3–§6) AND after its calibration corpora exist (§7.4/§7.4c, a Phase-4 precursor), so fix-space ⊇ defect-space and the daily gate has a corpus to stand on.**

### 7.1 Architecture: deterministic floor + LLM ceiling + calibrated first-person play + whole-game read + reference anchor

**Layer A — deterministic objective signals (cheap, hard-gating, no LLM).** Runs every iteration for ~free (pure Go, sub-second) and **must be clean before any paid LLM call** (§8).

| Tool | Package | What it computes | Would have caught |
|---|---|---|---|
| `luminance_report` | `internal/lumaudit` | luminance histogram, % black, over/under-exposure, contrast, palette | PolyWorld's 98%-black frames |
| `primitive_audit` | `internal/primitiveaudit` | scan for `/Engine/BasicShapes/*`, `BasicShapeMaterial`, `DrawDebugLine` | Aesir's cube turret + PolyWorld's cube lanes |
| `render_health` | Go over capture + config | static geometry rendered, boot/default map non-empty, `GameDefaultMap` → the game | PolyWorld's `static:0` + `GameDefaultMap=OpenWorld` |
| `feel_audit` | `internal/feelaudit` | per event (Tier-1): visual+audio+camera within N ms, under over-juice ceilings; input→response latency | Aesir's silent, flash-less debug-line turret |
| `verb_response` | `internal/feelaudit` | input→state-delta curve shape vs genre-preset envelope; degenerate-response flag | an untuned/teleport-snap/dead-zone core verb |
| `in_motion_audit` | `internal/feelaudit` | foot-slide + snap-rotation over a locomotion filmstrip | Aesir's foot-sliding, snap-rotating enemies |

The full-sweep non-degeneracy gate + fenced-corner detection also live in `balance_sweep`, not Layer A. Whole-game **through-line/cohesion** is NOT deterministic — it is a persona+human read, §7.4d. **Feel-QUALITY is NOT in Layer A** — Layer A certifies feel *presence*, not *quality*; quality is §7.4c's separate bet + the human feel-pulse.)*

These are the wedge: cheap, deterministic, testable, **self-validating** — Phase 0's acceptance is "run them against current PolyWorld and Aesir; they must fail for the exact reasons a human named," using the **Tier-1 heuristic.**

**Layer B — the LLM art/design critic (the ceiling).** `visual_critique` and `design_review` are **evidence-packagers**, not judges; judgment is delegated to specialist subagents with vision.
- **`visual_critique`** assembles: scene contact sheet + HUD captures + gameplay filmstrip + **high-rate feel bursts** + Layer-A signals + the **creative pillar + art bible + reference board** (§7.3), returning a **structured pairwise/scored critique request.** Scores presentation dimensions — *lighting, composition/focal-hierarchy, readability, art-direction coherence, material quality, feel-quality (presence-read only, quality deferred to §7.4c/pulse), spatial flow, distinctiveness/identity, "programmer art?"* — each with **named defects and named fixes that map to §2.2 tools.**
- **`design_review`** packages the filmstrip + feel bursts + audio track + state timeline + input log + **`decision_audit`/`balance_sweep`/`novelty_audit` outputs** + **the (calibration-gated) player-persona report (§7.4b–c)** for a **design + feel + audio + spatial** verdict: responsiveness, pacing, feedback readability, is-the-core-verb-satisfying, **decision density, dominant-strategy, progression, balance, content-variety/novelty-arc, fantasy-legibility**, spatial flow, objective readability, mix/audio identity, memorable moment. **`design_quality` is scored here and holds veto (§9); `novelty` and `fantasy-legibility` are veto sub-dimensions.**

**Layer C — the whole-game read (through-line/cohesion + reference anchor).** Above the per-axis scores. See §7.3 (anchor) and §7.4d (through-line — and its honest limit: it *detects* skin-deep incoherence; deep cohesion is bought at the pillar gate, not scored here).

### 7.2 Fixing the feel instrument — high-rate event-windowed capture (+ input-inject)

Game feel lives sub-100ms. Today `playtest_capture` samples at `interval_s` **default 0.5 = 2 fps** (`playtest_tools.go:45`), which cannot sample any of it. Fix:
- **Event-windowed 60fps bursts:** on a Tier-1-detected `fire`/`hit`/`death` event (and on movement-verb inputs, for `verb_response`), capture a **~500ms burst at 60fps.** The steady 2fps strip stays for the overview.
- **Input→visible-response latency as a hard metric** *and the verb-response curve* (§6.2) — deterministic numbers, not an eyeball.
- **Feel-quality gradient (a *hoped-for* path, §6.2/§7.4c):** IF the separate feel-quality persona bet clears its hardest gate, the calibrated persona drives frame-level input through the burst window via `input_inject` and scores feel-quality first-person. **This is not assumed; the default and permanent fallback is the human feel-pulse (§8b).**
- **A feel verdict from a 2fps strip must not gate a ship.**

### 7.3 The reference anchor — a *human-approved, mechanically-feasible creative pillar*, then bible, then pack demo, expressed in play

The difference between *competent* (6/10) and *good* (7+/10) is a strong creative hook, and that is the one thing an LLM is worst at self-judging. Fix the anchor hierarchy with a **human in it**, ensure the pillar is *expressed in play* not just in the look, **and — new (fix 4) — ensure the pillar's core fantasy is *mechanically reachable* before it is approved, so identity is never structurally skinned-then-vetoed:**
- **Primary anchor = a human-approved creative pillar** (`design_pillar`, authored up front, **human sign-off at the Phase-2 milestone**): exactly **one genre twist + one core fantasy.** Short, opinionated, human-owned.
- **Secondary anchor = the art bible** (Art-Director-authored, Phase 2) — the *look* that **serves** the pillar.
- **Tertiary anchor = the pack demo (`pack_orient`)** — a "this is achievable-good" **floor**, not the target.
- **`reference_board`** holds all three plus the calibration ladders (§7.4).

**The Phase-2 PILLAR-FEASIBILITY GATE (new — fix 4; closes the §7.3↔§6.1b contradiction).** Draft 5 authored the pillar *freely* while §6.1b/RC4 bound mechanics to pre-built scaffolds, and **nothing required the pillar's core fantasy to be a reachable mechanic.** The consequence was structural: an ambitious pillar whose fantasy is a *mechanic no scaffold builds* would ship as **skin** (LUT/fog/names via `look_author`/`theme_author`) — and then trip the through-line veto — so the studio would systematically **skin-then-veto its most identity-bearing pillars**, and cohesion would end up human-produced (re-pillar), not agent-produced. The gate fixes this at the human sign-off:

> **At the Phase-2 pillar sign-off, a pillar is APPROVED only if its core fantasy maps to EITHER (a) an existing scaffold axis (fantasy expressible *as* a baked mechanic), OR (b) an explicitly-scheduled senior-authored scaffold on the roadmap (a new axis, costed in §8, path (b) of RC4's escalation). A pillar whose core fantasy is a mechanic that is neither reachable now nor scheduled is NOT approved as-is — it is re-pillared until it is mechanically reachable.** This makes identity *mechanically reachable* — the fantasy is expressed **as a mechanic, not a skin** — which (per fix 6) is the **only** way agent-side deep cohesion becomes possible.

**Worked examples (the gate in action):**
- **Scaffold-reachable flagship (path a) — the new default example.** *"You are the last salvage crew of a dead colony; every shot you fire is scrap you can't spend to rebuild — hold the line by choosing what to sacrifice."* The core fantasy (desperate triage under siege, scarcity as the emotional core) maps **directly onto the reference scaffold's two axes**: *expand-vs-bank* **IS** "spend scrap to rebuild vs hoard it to survive the next spike" (the sacrifice is literal, mechanical), and *single-target-vs-AoE* **IS** "answer the armored breachers vs the swarm — you can't do both." The mechanic *is* the fantasy; `theme_author` then skins the fiction (salvage/colony/breachers), `look_author` the grade — but the decision already carries the identity. **Passes the gate via (a).**
- **The lighthouse pillar (path b) — kept as the worked example of a *scheduled* scaffold.** *"Wave defense where your turrets are your only light source in the dark / you are the last lighthouse keeper."* The core fantasy — *illumination itself is your defense* — is a **light-as-resource mechanic that no wave-defense scaffold builds** (single-target/AoE × expand/bank has no light axis). Under the old plan this shipped as fog + a LUT + renamed objectives (skin) and then failed the through-line veto. Under the gate, it is approved **only if the roadmap schedules a senior-authored "illumination-vs-firepower" scaffold** — a new axis (*place-light vs place-damage*; the flipping state is **darkness-spread / spawn-from-dark direction**, controlled by a `UDarknessDirectorComponent`, orthogonal by construction). Absent that scheduled scaffold, the lighthouse pillar is **re-pillared, not skinned-then-vetoed.** This is exactly how the gate turns identity from a doomed skin into either a reachable mechanic or an honestly-scheduled one.

**Distinctiveness gates on fantasy legibility AND through-line, not only look-coherence.** Draft 3's distinctiveness reduced to *art-direction* distinctiveness — a look. It splits three ways:
- **Look-coherence** — judged by the calibrated visual critic against the bible/pillar (ladder-gated, §7.4).
- **Fantasy-legibility** — *could a blind player state the core fantasy from playing?* — authored by `theme_author` (§6.4) + `asset_repurpose` (§3.6) **on top of a mechanically-reachable pillar**, reported by the **persona/human** (§7.4b, §8b).
- **Through-line/cohesion** — *does it read as one deliberate game?* (§7.4d). **Distinctiveness passes only when look-coherence, fantasy-legibility, AND through-line clear.** A gorgeous LUT with no legible fantasy, or 8s-across-the-board that don't cohere, is *not* distinct. And because deep cohesion is bought at the feasibility gate, **the single biggest determinant of a passing through-line is a mechanically-reachable pillar approved up front — not an agent fix downstream.**

### 7.4 Calibration — the keystone gate, and its CORPUS costed as a human workstream

The entire ceiling rests on the LLM critic reliably **ordering the mid-quality band.** Rebuilt:
- **(a) Pairwise elicitation, primary.** The critic's primary task is **A-vs-B ordering against ladder anchors**, not absolute 0–100. Spearman falls out of pairwise wins. Keep 0–100 as a **secondary readout.**
- **(b) Per-genre ladders, ≥20–30 scenes PER dimension.** A powered ladder **per dimension** (lighting, composition, readability, feel-quality-*presence*, audio, spatial, **distinctiveness**) **per genre band**, bad → mediocre → competent → pro, including genuinely-good shipped scenes.
- **(c) Held-out validation.** A slice of each ladder is **held out.** The gate is Spearman ≥ 0.7 **on held-out scenes, per dimension.**
- **(d) Explicit partial-failure fork.** The critic ships as a **hard quality gate only on dimensions that clear their held-out ladder.** Others stay **human-in-loop or diagnostic.** The trusted set is a **versioned, logged artifact** (`Saved/Critiques/calibration.json`).
- **(e) Abstain / escalate.** High score dispersion on a scene → **abstain and escalate to a human.**
- **(f) Distinctiveness is not exempt.** On the ladder with bad→pro anchors **against the pillar**, held to the same gate. **Until it clears, diagnostic only**; the identity leg is carried by the **persona/human fantasy-legibility + through-line read**, never an uncalibrated LLM aesthetic score.
- **(g) Ownership — MAINTAIN, not AUTHOR.** The Art Director agent **maintains/re-calibrates** ladders — but **must not AUTHOR the corpus it is graded on** (circular). The corpus is built by a **named human** (§7.4-corpus).

**(§7.4-corpus) The VISUAL calibration-corpus construction workstream — costed and scheduled.** Per-genre held-out ladders of 20–30 human-ranked scenes *per dimension per genre* are plausibly **hundreds of curated, human-quality-ranked reference scenes.**
- **A costed, scheduled Phase-4 PRECURSOR milestone** with a **NAMED HUMAN owner.**
- **Sources:** pack demo scenes + **real shipped games** + **deliberately-degraded variants** (over/under-exposed, cube-swapped, style-clashed, de-juiced).
- **The labor line is human pairwise ranking** of those scenes. **Priced in §8.**
- **Phase 4 START is gated on the visual corpus existing at powered size.** The Art Director may *maintain* it thereafter; never author the rungs it is graded on.

The first *seed* anchors are minted by the Phase 0.5 and 0.6 vertical slices; the *powered* corpus is this workstream. **The PERSONA ladder is costed SEPARATELY (§7.4c), and FEEL-QUALITY is a THIRD, still-more-expensive, most-likely-to-never-clear dataset (§7.4c).**

### 7.4b First-person play — the player-persona agent, and the delight-vs-retention split

`design_review` over a scripted capture is **replay-scoring**; fun is first-person. Add a **player-persona agent** that actually *plays*:
- **How it plays (grounded in the real surface).** The persona runs a closed **observe → decide → act** loop over the *semantic action surface*: it reads state via `pie_observe` (`gamestate.*` reflected snake_case, `counts.<class>`, `perf.*`), **chooses its next action toward a stated goal**, and acts by invoking the game's `BlueprintCallable` verbs (`ArmTool`/`LinkLane`/`Fire`/`Buy`/…) — the same exec channel `playtest_capture` beats use, but **driven by live decisions, not a fixed script.** **With `input_inject` (§6.2, Phase 3B) it *can* extend to frame-level input** — the *mechanical precondition* for an in-loop feel read; the semantic-verb loop is the buildable-today floor for *decision* and *compulsion* reads.
- **What it reports — TWO distinct legs, not one (fixes the retention≠delight major):**
  - **RETENTION / compulsion:** where it got bored (which minute/wave), what was unclear, what it never used, **whether it wanted a second session and *why*.**
  - **DELIGHT / mastery / surprise** *(distinct)*: did it hit a moment of **earned satisfaction** (a plan that paid off), **genuine surprise** (a reveal it didn't see coming — feeds `setpiece_author`'s experiential read), or a **mastery breakthrough** (it got visibly better at the verb)? **Retention and delight are orthogonal — F2P maxes retention with zero delight — so they are TWO gates.** A loop can be sticky and joyless; that fails the delight gate even if it passes retention.
  - Plus **the one change** it wanted, **whether it could state the core fantasy** (fantasy-legibility, §7.3), and **whether it read as one game** (through-line, §7.4d). Persona variants (rusher, optimizer, explorer, cautious) surface dominant strategies a single script never would.
- **FEEL-QUALITY is explicitly NOT one of the persona's default legs.** Boredom/retention/delight have *behavioral-trace correlates* an LLM can plausibly learn to read; **weight/snap/juice-landing do not** (an LLM watching a filmstrip of its own inputs cannot proprioceptively feel them). So feel-quality is carved out as a **separate, hardest bet (§7.4c)** and, by default, the **human feel-pulse's** job — not folded into this persona's report.
- **Where it feeds — *only after it is calibrated* (§7.4c).** Its report is a first-class input to `design_quality`, novelty, fantasy-legibility, through-line, **retention**, and **delight** — **but an LLM cannot feel boredom or delight; it confabulates.** So: **until it clears its calibration ladder (§7.4c), its output is DIAGNOSTIC only**, and the retention/delight/fantasy legs are carried by the periodic human pulse (§8b). It is **additional to**, never a replacement for, the deterministic audits and the scripted regression capture.

### 7.4c Persona calibration — SEPARATE research bets with a PERMANENT fallback, and FEEL-QUALITY carved out as the hardest *(rebuilt — fixes the CTO's persona-corpus major AND the gamedev's feel-quality major)*

The persona carries the *"is it fun / will they return / did it delight"* load, so it gets the **same rigor as the visual critic — but it is a fundamentally more expensive and riskier dataset than the visual corpus, and feel-quality is more expensive and riskier still.** These are **three separately-costed bets, ranked by likelihood of never clearing**, not one:

**Bet 1 — RETENTION/DELIGHT persona calibration (expensive; may clear per-genre).**
- **A held-out persona ladder of loops/sessions with KNOWN human outcomes.** Seeded from the two baseline projects + public titles + deliberately-degraded variants (a de-tuned metronome loop, a novelty-starved loop, a sticky-but-joyless loop), each with **real human labels: boredom-point, second-session (retention), AND earned-satisfaction/surprise/mastery (delight).**
- **This is NOT scene-ranking — it is human PLAY-AND-REPORT sessions.** A visual rung is "look at a screenshot and rank it" (minutes). A persona rung is "a human plays a full loop/session and reports where they got bored, whether they'd return, and whether it delighted them" (tens of minutes to hours per rung, per subject, and you need *multiple subjects per rung* for a reliable label). **A different order of cost — costed separately in §8 as headcount × weeks × subject count, not amortized into the visual corpus.**
- **The gate:** boredom-point prediction (Spearman) + second-session prediction (AUC) + **delight-event prediction (AUC)** must correlate with the human labels **per genre**, symmetric to the critic's held-out Spearman.

**Bet 2 — FEEL-QUALITY persona calibration (the MOST expensive and MOST-likely-to-never-clear bet; carved out per fix 5).**
- **A SEPARATE held-out ladder of the SAME verb at graded feel quality** (mushy → floaty → weighty → crisp/pro), each rung carrying **human FELT labels** — real players rating *how the verb feels to control* after driving it, not watching it.
- **Its OWN gate:** the persona's feel-quality ordering must clear a **held-out AUC/Spearman ≥ its threshold against the human *felt* labels, per genre** — a gate *distinct from* Bet 1's, because the target signal (proprioceptive feel) is categorically harder to predict from any observable trace than boredom is.
- **Ranked the MOST-likely-to-never-clear bet in the entire plan.** Predicting a human's *felt* sense of weight/snap from an LLM reading a 60fps burst of its own injected inputs is, candidly, the research bet we expect to fail most often. We fund it because the payoff (an in-loop feel gradient) is large, but we **plan for it never clearing.**
- **`input_inject` is its precondition, not its guarantee.** Landing `input_inject` (§6.2) is necessary for this bet to even be *attempted*; it does nothing to make the bet *succeed*. We do not present "input_inject + persona feel-quality" as the expected in-loop outcome — it is a hoped-for one.

**PERMANENT fallbacks (not temporary bridges).** For every bet that does not clear on a genre:
- **Retention/delight:** the **human pulse (§8b)** carries compulsion, delight, and fantasy **INDEFINITELY** on that genre. "The compulsion/delight legs gate in-loop" is **strictly contingent on Bet 1 passing** — if it never passes, those legs are *permanently* human-milestone-gated, an accepted, planned-for end state.
- **Feel-quality:** the **periodic human feel-pulse (§8b)** carries verb-feel-quality **INDEFINITELY and by default** — this is **how real studios ship feel** and is the plan's expected steady state on most genres, not a fallback we're embarrassed by. The in-loop feel gradient is the *upside case*, not the baseline.

The studio still ships good games in every fallback case; it just never gets that in-loop gradient on that genre and pays the human-pulse cadence for it forever.
- **Ownership:** QA/Player-Persona agent maintains/re-calibrates all three ladders; a human owns the ground-truth labels. Maintains but does not author.

**Persona-vs-human agreement is tracked on the §8b milestone panel** — the running proof (or disproof) that each instrument is trustworthy before we let it drive, with **feel-quality tracked as its own line** so its (expected) lag is visible, not hidden inside a blended persona number.

### 7.4d The whole-game through-line — DETECTED, not GENERATED *(fix 6, stated plainly)*

A veto **above** the per-axis scores: *"is this ONE deliberate game or a bundle of competent parts?"* Read by **persona (diagnostic) + human (gating)**; **it can fail a build that is 8s across every per-axis dimension.** But we are now precise about what this veto can and cannot do:

- **What it DETECTS (its real, valuable job):** *skin-deep* incoherence — a grade that fights the fiction, naming that fights the theme, a setpiece that doesn't pay off, a look bolted onto an unrelated loop. This is genuinely catchable by a persona/human read of the whole build.
- **What its FIX-SPACE reaches (skin-deep only):** re-anchor to the pillar via `theme_author`/`look_author`/`asset_repurpose` + re-tune. These are the agent-reachable fixes, and they resolve skin-deep incoherence.
- **What it CANNOT generate — and what the toolchain cannot author at all:** **DEEP** mechanical-thematic cohesion — feel + fiction + systems + look all expressing **ONE idea because the mechanic itself IS the fantasy.** That comes from mechanics expressing the fantasy, and (RC4) the toolchain cannot author mechanics. **So deep cohesion is NOT an agent-satisfiable quality gate.** It is **human-authored, and its one enabling lever is the pillar-feasibility gate (§7.3):** a *mechanically-reachable pillar approved up front* is the **only** way an agent-built game can be deeply cohesive, because it is the only point at which the fantasy is made a mechanic rather than a skin. Downstream, the through-line veto can *detect* the absence of deep cohesion, but it cannot *manufacture* it — a build whose pillar was a skin from the start will fail the veto, and the honest fix is **upstream re-pillaring by a human**, not a downstream agent tool.

The through-line veto's honest role, stated once and for all: **"catches skin-deep incoherence and drives its agent-reachable fix; DEEP cohesion is human-authored via the pillar-feasibility gate, and is enabled up front, never generated downstream."**

### 7.5 Metric design — demote utilization, gate design + presence-of-good + through-line, validate the proxy, anti-hack

- **`asset_utilization` is a diagnostic, not a gate.** Gameable and inverts under scrutiny. Ship-gate replacement: `primitive_audit` clean in **hero/structural slots** + a **critic-scored composition/density dimension.**
- **Design-quality metrics (design ceiling), gated:** `decision_audit`, `balance_sweep` (win-rate/stability/dominance/degeneracy/progression/**full-sweep axis-liveness + fenced-corner set**), `novelty_audit`, and the **calibration-gated** persona retention/delight/fantasy signal. **`design_quality` holds veto; `novelty` and `fantasy-legibility` are veto sub-dimensions.**
- **Presence-of-good metrics (presentation ceiling), gated where calibrated:** composition/focal-hierarchy, memorable-moment (craft floor + persona/human experiential read), distinctiveness (look-coherence *and* fantasy-legibility *and* through-line; diagnostic until all clear). **Feel-QUALITY is NOT gated here — it is the human feel-pulse (§8b) by default, persona-in-loop only where Bet 2 clears (§7.4c).**
- **Whole-game through-line/cohesion — a veto ABOVE the per-axis scores:** read by **persona (diagnostic) + human (gating)**; **it can fail a build that is 8s across every per-axis dimension.** Its fix-space is **skin-deep only** (§7.4d); **deep cohesion is enabled at the pillar gate (§7.3), not fixed here.**
- **The design ceiling is a PROXY STACK — validate it against the north-star, and BEFORE the generative-reach claim (fix 3).** The blind-human ≥7/10 + second-session + **delight** is the outcome; the audits + persona are *proxies.* **A proxy-validation milestone (§11 Phase 5, sequenced FIRST within Phase 5): on N built games, correlate the design-proxy stack against the blind-human north-star; report the correlation.** Low correlation → **fix the proxies, not the games.** **Until validated, the design ceiling is labeled an UNVALIDATED PROXY — and `design_explore` may not claim "better," only "proxy-distinct, for human ratification" (§6.5).**
- **Anti-reward-hacking:** judge **pixels + audio + play-trace + sim-statistics, not code**; **deterministic floor under every subjective score**; **pillar + reference anchored + fully logged**; **held-out pairwise calibration** for the critic *and* each persona bet. If the agent games it, the fix is a better *anchor*, not a weaker gate.

### 7.6 The closed loop

**`critique_iterate`** (Go orchestrator):
```
generate → capture (2fps overview + 60fps feel/verb bursts + audio track) + balance_sweep (headless if struct-factored, else on-lease; incl. full-sweep non-degeneracy gate + fenced-corner clamps)
         → Layer-A audits (luminance, primitive, render_health, feel, verb_response, in_motion, audio, decision, novelty, style_cohesion)  [free/cheap, every iter]
         → if any hard-fail: apply the mapped fix (§2.2), re-capture, repeat        [no paid vision call]
         → ONLY when Layer A is clean: visual_critique / design_review (Layer B, pairwise vs pillar+bible+refs)  [paid]
         → player-persona play pass → feeds design_quality/novelty/fantasy/retention/DELIGHT/through-line IF calibrated, else DIAGNOSTIC + periodic human pulse
              (feel-QUALITY: human feel-pulse by default; persona gradient only where Bet 2 cleared)
         → whole-game THROUGH-LINE read (persona diagnostic + human gating) — can VETO an 8s-across-the-board-but-incoherent build (skin-deep fixes agent-side; deep incoherence → human re-pillar)
         → if any GATED dimension < bar (per §7.4d/§7.4c fork): apply the ranked, named fixes (each a §2.2 tool call)
              → re-capture → re-critique
         → until all gated dimensions pass OR per-artifact budget hit (§8) → then escalate to a human
```
**The fix-action vocabulary is enumerated in §2.2** — every ranked fix is a concrete tool call **inside the agent's authority**; structural-mechanic gaps, deep-incoherence, and unreachable-fantasy escalate to a human (or motivate a scheduled scaffold / a `design_explore` proposal, §6.5/§7.3). **The gate is not "≥70 everywhere"** — it is: all Layer-A clean **and** the presentation ceiling at its bar **and** the **design ceiling (decisions + balance + progression + novelty + retention + delight) at its bar** **and** the **whole-game through-line clears** — with distinctiveness/fantasy/feel-quality and the persona-driven legs gating only where they cleared calibration.

## 8. ECONOMICS + THROUGHPUT — priced on the real bottleneck, at whole-game scale, with ALL THREE human/senior workstreams QUANTITATIVELY costed *(rewritten — fix 2)*

**The bottleneck is PIE capture wall-clock, not vision cost**; a game is dozens of scenes + systems; the concurrency claim rests on **MP6** (pending, task #18); **and the three biggest one-time human/senior costs — the visual calibration corpus, the separately-more-expensive persona ladder (incl. the hardest feel-quality bet), AND senior-design scaffold authoring — must all be quantitatively costed, not hand-waved.** Re-anchored. **Numbers are engineering estimates to validate, not measured facts.**

**Design principle — minimize the bottleneck, not just the token.** Layer A gates for ~free; the loop **only escalates to paid vision when Layer A is clean** — but the scarce resource is the **editor lease** (capture), so the real levers are *fewer captures* and *skip-recapture caching.*

**Per-iteration cost/latency (estimate) — capture wall-clock is the primary driver:**

| Stage | Wall-clock | $ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PIE capture (2fps + bursts + audio)** | **~30–60s** | ~0 | **editor-lease-bound; the true bottleneck** |
| Apply fixes (editor ops) | seconds–minutes | ~0 | editor-lease-bound |
| Layer A (all audits) | <2s | ~0 | pure Go, every iteration, off-lease |
| `balance_sweep` (struct-factored) | seconds–minutes | ~0 | **no editor, no vision; massively parallel — ONLY if the loop obeys the §6.1 BUILD RULE** |
| `balance_sweep` (NOT struct-factored) | ~capture-bound | ~0 | **runs via simulate mode → COMPETES for the editor lease** |
| `design_explore` (MAP-Elites, struct-factored) | minutes–hours (batch) | ~0–low | **off-lease if BUILD-RULE-factored; E evaluations × cheap struct sim; persona-vet adds small text-LLM $ where calibrated (§6.5)** |
| Player-persona play pass | ~capture-bound | ~0–low | exec/observe loop; small text LLM, no vision |
| LLM vision critique | ~10–30s | ~$0.05–0.30 | only when Layer A clean; **the secondary constraint** |

**The `balance_sweep`/`design_explore` off-lease claim is true BY CONSTRUCTION, not by optimism.** $0/off-lease **only** for loops whose sim obeys the §6.1 BUILD RULE (engine-free seedable struct + free step, mirroring `FPolyResolvedCatalog`). The common case today is world-bound (`APolyDirector` is an `AActor`); those sweep through simulate mode and **compete for the editor lease.** The BUILD RULE *earns* the cheap path — and the cheap path is what makes the MAP-Elites search (§6.5) affordable at E evaluations.

**Per-scene envelope, honestly.** 5–8 iterations × (capture + level-load + fix-apply) ≈ **~15–40 min wall-clock per scene**, capture-dominated. Vision $ over that ≈ ~$1–2. **Throughput ≈ ~1.5–4 scenes/editor/hour** — capture-bound. Report throughput in **scenes/day/editor**, vision $ secondary.

**Whole-game budget.** A game is dozens of scenes + systems + balance passes + re-pacing:
- **Critical-path-first schedule:** converge the vertical-slice scene + core loop first; dress secondary scenes after.
- **Skip-recapture cache:** an unchanged scene is **not re-captured**; a scene-hash gates re-capture.
- **Systems/balance + generative search iterate off-editor — *only for struct-factored loops*.** Non-factored loops queue for the lease.

**The THREE named human/senior-labor workstreams — ALL quantitatively costed (none invisible, none an agent task):**

1. **The VISUAL calibration corpus (§7.4-corpus):** hundreds of human pairwise-ranked scenes. **Estimate:** ~20–30 scenes × ~7 dimensions × ~4 genre bands ≈ **~600–850 ranked scenes**; pairwise ranking at minutes-per-comparison ≈ **~3–5 person-weeks** of a trained ranker, plus curation/degradation authoring. **Gates Phase 4 start.**

2. **The PERSONA ladders (§7.4c) — costed SEPARATELY, and split into TWO bets:**
   - **Bet 1 (retention/delight):** human **play-and-report sessions** — tens of minutes to hours per rung, **multiple subjects per rung** (≥3 for a reliable label), per genre. **Estimate:** ~15–20 rungs/genre × ~3 subjects × ~1 hr/session × 4 genres ≈ **~180–240 session-hours** ≈ **~6–10 person-weeks** incl. recruitment/analysis — **a bigger line than the visual corpus.**
   - **Bet 2 (feel-quality) — the hardest, separately costed:** graded-feel ladders of the *same verb* with human FELT labels, per genre. **Estimate:** similar session-hours to Bet 1 **plus** the cost of *authoring* graded-feel variants of each verb (senior-feel labor) — call it **~6–10 person-weeks**, with the honest caveat that **it may never clear**, so its true cost includes the **permanent human feel-pulse cadence** (below).
   - Both gate only the *in-loop* fun/feel gradient, not the ability to ship.

3. **Reference-scaffold authoring (§6.1b) — senior-DESIGN labor, QUANTITATIVELY costed (fix 2), symmetric to the persona ladder:**
   - **The price is per-axis-pair authoring-and-proving:** designing a scaffold's ≥2 axes so their optima genuinely shift with *controlled* state, then proving it under the full-sweep gate (or reaching ≥85% + fencing the collapse corners). **Estimate per scaffold:** the **Phase-0.6 reference ≈ ~3 senior-designer-weeks** (design 2 axes + prove full-sweep + template it); **each subsequent genre scaffold ≈ ~1.5–2 senior-designer-weeks.**
   - **Library target:** **~4 systems genres** (wave-defense, tycoon, economy, arena-management) × **~2–3 axes each.** So a 4-scaffold seed library ≈ **3 + 3×1.75 ≈ ~8–9 senior-designer-weeks** — the **same order as the persona ladder**, a named, scheduled senior-design workstream, **not a free agent task.**
   - **Reference-first amortization is the MITIGATION, NOT the price.** The Phase-0.6 reference is the *template*; subsequent scaffolds fork its proven struct-shape + full-sweep harness, which is why the marginal scaffold is ~1.5–2 weeks rather than ~3. **But the gate must still be re-satisfied per scaffold** — amortization lowers the marginal cost, it does not eliminate the per-scaffold proof. We price the *proof*, and *credit* the amortization; we never price the amortization as if it were free authoring.
   - **The satisfiability risk is a named cost line (fix 2).** Some genres' axes may not stay live across 100% of the grid; the **fenced fallback** (≥85% + clamped collapse-corners, §6.1b) is what keeps the workstream *satisfiable* — but a scaffold that cannot reach ≥85% even with fencing is **rejected and re-designed**, and that rework is budgeted as a **risk buffer (~+30% on the scaffold line)**, not assumed away.
   - **This is `design_explore`'s fuel (§6.5).** Operator-1 (cross-transplant) is inert until L≥3 scaffolds exist, so the search's reach is *paced by this workstream's throughput* — the plan's scarcest, most expensive fuel, now costed as such.

Plus the recurring **human-pulse cadence** (§8b): on any genre where Bet 1 never clears, a periodic real-player session carries compulsion/delight; **and on essentially all genres by default, the human FEEL-pulse carries feel-quality — indefinitely.** These are standing operational costs, not one-time gates.

**Studio concurrency — gated on MP6, with a SCHEDULED single-editor branch.**
> **If MP6 is green by Phase 5:** N editors; north-star throughput concurrent; off-lease work (struct sweeps, `design_explore`, critique, calibrated-persona passes) fans out freely.
> **If MP6 slips:** Phase 5 runs **single-editor**; off-lease work **still fans out**; the north-star milestone is reached **serially, later, at the same quality bar.** MP6 is a **decision point at the Phase-5 boundary**, not a silent dependency.

**Metric partitioning — CI vs periodic.**
- **(a) CI gates — every iteration:** all Layer-A audits, the playtest rubric, input→response latency + verb-response envelope, `decision_audit`, `novelty_audit`, `balance_sweep` targets (incl. full-sweep non-degeneracy + fenced-corner clamps), and the **calibrated-and-cleared** LLM critic + persona dimensions. **The daily operational gate is the LLM critic + calibrated persona — which is exactly why all three corpora are hard prerequisites.**
- **(b) Periodic human milestones (the §8b panel) — not in the loop:** blind A/B studies; the human-artist agreement panel; the shipped-dev preference test; **feel-QUALITY + verb-feel-QUALITY validation (a PERMANENT human feel-pulse by default; the in-loop persona gradient is the upside case, not the baseline — Bet 2, §7.4c)**; the Phase-2 pillar sign-off **incl. the pillar-feasibility gate**; **the persona-vs-human agreement track (retention AND delight AND feel-quality as separate lines, §7.4c)**; **the whole-game through-line human read**; **the design-proxy-vs-north-star correlation (§7.5, sequenced before the reach claim)**; and — on any genre where Bet 1 hasn't cleared — **the compulsion/delight/fantasy human pulse on the critical-path loop, indefinitely.**

## 12. Risks, non-goals, honest limits

- **Generation is a MEASURED SEARCH specified as MAP-Elites, not a stub (the draft-4 blocker; specified to build-depth per fix 1).** `design_explore` (§6.5) maintains a **behavior-descriptor archive** (decision-cadence-entropy × win-rate-slope × novelty-profile, 5×5×4=100 cells), proposes via three concrete operators (cross-scaffold axis transplant with a named regime-mutation generator, within-scaffold regime mutation, asset re-purposing), vets against the *same* audits + calibrated persona that judge a hand-built game, and surfaces the **top K=8** viable-and-behaviorally-distinct elites. It has a **falsifiable unit test** (rediscover a planted off-seed regime) and **quantified, expensive fuel** (§8). **It reaches toward VARIETY — the edges of the authored design space — NOT toward originality; a new verb is net-new C++ (RC4) and is excluded. The ceiling is a reliable *good* with a reach toward variety — never a guarantee of *great*, and scoped to systems/decision-driven genres.**
- **The scaffold library is `design_explore`'s scarcest fuel and is QUANTITATIVELY costed (the CTO major; fix 2).** Scaffold authoring is priced as **senior-designer headcount × weeks × #genres × axes-per-genre** (§8: ~3 wk for the reference, ~1.5–2 wk each subsequent, ~8–9 senior-designer-weeks for a 4-scaffold seed library), with **reference-first amortization as the *mitigation*, not the price.** Operator-1 (cross-transplant) is **inert until L≥3 scaffolds exist**, so the search's reach is *paced by this workstream.*
- **The keystone scaffold is DESIGNED and PROVEN, with a SATISFIABLE gate (the draft-4 blocker; fix 2).** Phase 0.6 designs one full-sweep-proven reference scaffold; every shipped scaffold carries the **full-sweep non-degeneracy gate — with a fenced fallback: ≥85% grid-liveness + collapse-corners CLAMPED so the agent literally cannot tune into a metronome.** The 100%-liveness ideal may be unsatisfiable; the fenced rule is what ships. The degenerate turret range-vs-firerate example is replaced with single-target-vs-AoE × expand-vs-bank, orthogonal *by construction* because the flipping state (wave composition + spike timing) is scaffold-controlled.
- **Phase-5 ordering is fixed: proxy-validation BEFORE the reach claim (the CTO major; fix 3).** `design_explore` uses `balance_sweep`/`novelty_audit` as both fitness and diversity descriptors, and those are an **unvalidated proxy** until §7.5's milestone. So within Phase 5 the proxy-validation runs **first**; until it clears, `design_explore` surfaces **balance-vetted-but-taste-UNVETTED** candidates only — the same discipline as the uncalibrated persona.
- **Identity is made MECHANICALLY REACHABLE at a pillar-feasibility gate (the gamedev blocker; fix 4).** At the Phase-2 human sign-off, a pillar is approved only if its core fantasy maps to **an existing scaffold axis OR a scheduled senior-authored scaffold** — closing the §7.3↔§6.1b contradiction so the studio no longer structurally **skins-then-vetoes** its most ambitious pillars. The flagship example is now scaffold-reachable (scavenger-siege = expand/bank × single-target/AoE); the lighthouse pillar is kept as the worked example of a *scheduled* (path-b) scaffold.
- **Feel-quality is a SEPARATE, hardest research bet with a PERMANENT human feel-pulse (the gamedev major; fix 5).** Feel-quality-persona-calibration is carved out of general persona calibration with its **own held-out AUC/Spearman gate against human FELT labels**, **ranked the most-likely-to-never-clear bet.** `input_inject` (a prioritized Phase-3B op; the current `input_action`/`input_axis` are offline `.ini` mappers, `affordances.go:38-39`) is its **precondition, never its guarantee.** The **human feel-pulse is the named PERMANENT fallback and the expected steady state** — the in-loop feel gradient is the *upside case*, not the baseline. We stop implying `input_inject`+persona is the expected outcome.
- **Cohesion is DETECTED, not GENERATED (the gamedev major; fix 6).** The through-line veto **catches skin-deep incoherence** (grade/fiction/naming) and drives its agent-reachable fix; **DEEP mechanical-thematic cohesion — the mechanic BEING the fantasy — is NOT agent-generatable** (RC4) and is **human-authored, enabled UP FRONT at the pillar-feasibility gate**, never manufactured downstream. A build whose pillar was a skin from the start fails the veto and needs human re-pillaring, not an agent tool.
- **The design ceiling is SCOPED to simulable/systems genres (the CTO major).** Craft/execution genres are **out of scope for the "good" claim** until the non-simulable de-risk slice (Phase 5, gated on `input_inject`) passes. We commit to the scope and name the single path to lift it.
- **All THREE human/senior workstreams are quantitatively costed, and a DEPENDENCY DAG names the longest pole (fix 7).** The visual corpus, the two persona bets, and senior scaffold authoring are priced in §8; the DAG (§11.0) shows the **longest pole is the human/senior workstreams (weeks-to-never), not the engine spikes** — and forces front-loading them and splitting the overloaded Phase 3 into 3A (systems + design-quality) and 3B (feel + audio + fiction).

Every instrument is calibrated before it gates; the whole proxy stack is validated against the north-star before the reach claim; **until validated, it is an unvalidated proxy.**
- **Dominance detection is measurable, not magical.** `decision_audit` claims only density + cadence; dominance lives in `balance_sweep` with a **named policy generator**, caveated **declared, not emergent** (§6.1b).
- **`balance_sweep` and `design_explore` are off-lease/free BY CONSTRUCTION** — only for BUILD-RULE-factored struct economies (mirroring `FPolyResolvedCatalog`); world-bound loops (`APolyDirector` is an `AActor`) run through simulate mode and **compete for the editor lease**, priced as such (§8).
- **`asset_thumbnail`, retarget, audio capture, AND `input_inject` are the engine-integration bets — all spec'd/spiked, not hand-waved** (and, per the DAG, *not* the longest pole).
- **Event detection is two-tier and never assumed** (Tier-1 heuristic floor + Tier-2 instrumented).
- **`kit_compose` is bounded + fallback-first; `mesh_semantics` emits a confidence, not a binary.**
- **Economics are priced on capture wall-clock, at whole-game scale, with ALL THREE workstreams costed and concurrency gated on MP6 with a scheduled single-editor fallback.** A hard per-artifact budget (~8 iters / ~$2 vision) escalates to a human; skip-recapture caching concentrates budget on scenes that moved.
- **Non-goals:** frame-accurate determinism; many-agents-per-editor (until MP6); **AnimBP / Niagara / audio *graph* authoring** — strictly via **instancing** existing pack systems + `User.*` params; **inventing net-new mechanics / new verbs in a structurally decision-poor loop** (a human-in-loop scaffold change, or a `design_explore` proposal a human ratifies — not an autonomous agent task, and never "originality"); **craft/execution genres until the non-simulable slice passes**; **agent-generated deep mechanical-thematic cohesion** (enabled at the human pillar gate, not generated); shipping-store/marketing.
- **The deepest risks are reward-hacking the critic, optimizing a proxy that doesn't predict the outcome, a generative search that converges back to the seed, AND presenting hoped-for research bets (persona feel-quality) as expected outcomes.** Addressed structurally: judge pixels+audio+play-trace+sim-statistics not code; deterministic floor under every subjective score; pillar + reference anchor + the feasibility gate; human-auditable logs; held-out calibration of both critic and each persona bet; proxy-vs-north-star validation *before* the reach claim; a **MAP-Elites archive** (§6.5) so the search returns a *spread* of viable-and-distinct candidates, not one local optimum; and **honest hedging of the feel-quality bet with a permanent human fallback.** If the agent games it, the fix is a better *anchor* (or a better *proxy*), not a weaker gate.
